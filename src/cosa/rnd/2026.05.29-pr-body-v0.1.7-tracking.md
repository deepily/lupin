## PR body (copy from here down)

This PR merges the complete **v0.1.7** development cycle into `main` — 94 commits spanning
2026-04-24 → 2026-05-28. It is the largest CoSA merge to date (≈20.6k insertions across 108 files),

### 🚦 CJ Flow async multi-lane (the headline feature)

The CoSA Jobs Flow queue gained a true concurrent execution lane for long-running agentic jobs.

- **Agentic pool** — `AgenticJobBase` jobs now dispatch to a `ThreadPoolExecutor` sized by the
  `cj flow max concurrent agentic jobs` INI key; the consumer thread returns immediately and a
  `Future.add_done_callback` drives the done/dead transition. Fast-lane (`AgentBase` /
  `SolutionSnapshot`) work stays inline and is never blocked by the pool.
- **Thread safety** — `FifoQueue` now guards `queue_list` + `queue_dict` with a `threading.RLock`;
  all 9 `pop()` sites migrated to `delete_by_id_hash()` since head-of-queue is no longer
  deterministic under pool-callback concurrency.
- **Ghost-job sweeper** — daemon thread dead-letters jobs whose `Future` completed but never
  transitioned, on a configurable sweep interval.
- **ApiResourceManager** — new singleton centralizing per-provider rate-limit waits + call
  recording (Deep Research migrated; legacy per-agent retry paths documented as the second path).
- **Observability** — `GET /api/queue/pool-status` reports inflight/max workers, pending-in-pool,
  and ApiResourceManager state.
- **Error-path hardening** — dead-queue refactor, consumer heartbeat, and `do_all` re-raise
  propagated across 8 agent subclasses.

- `commons_rate_limiter` — per-user + global caps.
