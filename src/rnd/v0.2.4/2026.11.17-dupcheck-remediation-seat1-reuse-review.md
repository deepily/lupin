## Item 17, not done: make_role_interface_functions stays

The 14 Nov ruling d-0ec558ba84 (receipt 7afa66de44763a5d) says EXTEND: add a role mode to `make_interface_functions` and drop the second factory. The 13 Nov review (receipt dd1176b64142d8ce, search rs-b163e3087c, in `src/rnd/v0.2.3/2026.11.13-dupcheck-extend-fixes-reuse-review.md`) ruled the same pair DISTINCT. Reading both bodies again, the 13 Nov ruling holds, on three differences:

- Identity pushed: the plain factory copies SENDER_ID and TARGET_USER onto the dispatcher; the role factory copies SESSION_ID and TARGET_USER, because the role-aware dispatcher builds the sender from the role.
- Parameter order: the role wrappers take `role` as the second positional parameter, which `swe_team/orchestrator.py` relies on in `ask_confirmation( question, role, default, timeout, abstract )`; the plain wrappers have no role.
- Defaults and shape: `ask_confirmation` defaults to 120 s with no `job_id` or `priority` in the role factory and 60 s with both in the plain one; the role factory returns ( notify_progress, ask_confirmation, request_decision, get_feedback ) and the plain one ( notify_progress, ask_confirmation, get_feedback, present_choices ).

A role mode would hold both sets of wrapper bodies inside one function behind a flag, so no code would be shared and every caller's return order would still differ. Nothing was changed for item 17.
