# Audit: where the preference-learning code throws away part of Rick's answer

Cheech 🌿, 23 Nov 2026, read-only. Row `e889b0b9`, run on Rick's word by voice the same morning. No code was changed.
Measured on branch `wip-v0.2.4-2026.11.17-context-aware-online-preference-learning` at `679621e2b`, by reading the source. No database counts were taken today. Every count below comes from the 21 Nov add-on (`src/rnd/v0.2.4/2026.11.17-principle-learner-research/2026.11.21-addendum-yes-no-neither-review.md`) and is cited as such.

## Verdict

The answer is **stored whole and read back whole**: yes, no or neither, the comment, and the typed Other text all survive into the history and the case store. It is **lost later, at three kinds of step**:

1. **Every model forecaster can only reply yes or no**, or one of the listed options, so none of them can forecast a neither, a comment or a re-frame.
2. **The decision proxy's trust feedback reduces every answer to approved or not.** So a neither counts as a rejection, and a "yes, but…" counts as clean agreement. This path is **live**: it moves trust scores today.
3. **No code compares typed Other text with the options.** About a quarter of those answers are the recommended option plus a comment. They are either scored as misses, or left out of the score altogether.

Two pieces are already built and unused: the comparator computes whether the comments agree, and nothing reads it; and a three-way forecaster that can say neither runs in shadow.

## What I did not check

- Whether the three-way forecaster's shadow rows exist in the database, and how many there are. That needs a read-only query.
- Batch open-ended cards, and the paths that read answers for something other than forecasting: `runtime_argument_expeditor`, `podcast_proxy` and the stop hook's "anything else" ask. They read answers, but they forecast nothing.
- The hint card the browser shows. I did not trace it from the browser side, only from what the engine sends.

## Suggested order, if Rick says go

1. **The decision proxy (row 12).** It is small and live. A neither should stop counting against the proxy.
2. **The shared typed-answer labelling (row 1).** Rows 5, 9 and 11 and the re-run all need it. Label the stored typed answers once, then freeze the labels.
3. **The widened forecaster reply (row 7), and reading `qualifier_match` (row 8).** Together these are what the re-run (row `d3210b76`) measures.
4. **The lookup engine's neither forecast (row 4)** and **typed answers as votes (row 5)**.
5. Row 10 last, if at all.
