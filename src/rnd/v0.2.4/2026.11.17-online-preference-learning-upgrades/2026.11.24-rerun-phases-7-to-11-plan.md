## 4. Rick's V6 rule

A phase ruling stands unless its paired difference reverses sign under the new scoring. For each paired comparison the report prints the old difference and p, the new difference and p, both on ids that are decided in both, and `sign reversed`. A new difference of exactly 0 is "no sign", not a reversal. A reversal is a finding: it goes to Rick as one line (phase, comparison, both signs), no ruling is edited and nothing is acted on.
- Phase 8's ruling (not decisive against always-yes): tested on the challenger against always-yes. At the old figure the challenger said yes on all 215 final asks and tied always-yes ask for ask; the thing to watch is whether it still does.
- Phase 7: one pair, per-field live against control.
- Phases 9 and 11 carry no paired ruling in the old record that I found; they are reported as new columns with no sign test, and the report says so.
