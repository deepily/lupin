# Three-Model PEFT/LoRA Comparison for Agentic Intent Routing

**Date**: 2026-02-12
**Task**: Compare Ministral-8B, Qwen3-4B, and Llama-3.2-3B for 34-command agentic intent routing
**Status**: ANALYSIS COMPLETE
**Recommendation**: Deploy Ministral-8B 8-bit quantization (98.7% EM, lossless, ~9GB VRAM)

---

## Executive Summary

Three models were fine-tuned with LoRA on identical training data (34 agentic routing commands, ~1500 samples/command) and evaluated across 4 stages: PRE-training baseline, POST-training (full precision), POST-quantization (4-bit GPTQ), and POST-quantization (8-bit GPTQ, Ministral only).

**Key finding**: Ministral-8B-Instruct-2410 dominates on every axis that matters for production routing:
- **97.9% exact match POST-training** (vs 94.7% Qwen3, 76.1% Llama)
- **8-bit quantization is lossless**: 98.7% EM, **0 commands degraded**, +0.8pp vs full precision
- **4-bit quantization is viable but lossy**: 95.8% EM, 7 commands degraded, -2.1pp
- Qwen3-4B 4-bit loses 11.8pp on quantization (94.7% -> 83.0%, 20 commands degraded)
- Llama-3.2-3B 4-bit loses 12.0pp on quantization (76.1% -> 64.1%, 25 commands degraded)

**Deployment recommendation**: Switch development router from Qwen3-4B 4-bit to Ministral-8B 8-bit. The 8-bit model fits comfortably on a 24GB RTX 4090 at 0.85 GPU utilization (~8.7GB model + KV cache + embeddings = ~12-14GB, well under the 20.4GB ceiling).

## 2. Quantization Resilience Analysis

| Metric | Ministral-8B (8-bit) | Ministral-8B (4-bit) | Qwen3-4B (4-bit) | Llama-3.2-3B (4-bit) |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| EM delta vs POST-training | **+0.8pp** | -2.1pp | -11.8pp | -12.0pp |
| Commands degraded | **0 / 34** | 7 / 34 | 20 / 34 | 25 / 34 |
| Commands improved | 4 / 34 | 3 / 34 | 2 / 34 | 2 / 34 |
| Worst degradation | none | -21.4pp (calculator) | -42.9pp (auto route) | -50.0pp (kagi clip new) |
| Quantization speedup | 1.10x | 1.33x | 1.93x | 1.90x |

**Key insight**: 8-bit quantization on Ministral-8B is effectively **lossless** — it actually _gains_ 0.8pp over full precision, likely due to regularization effects from weight rounding. No command lost accuracy. Four commands improved (search new tab, date and time, receptionist, none — all +7.1pp).

**Why Qwen3 and Llama suffer at 4-bit**: Smaller models have less parameter redundancy. Aggressive 4-bit quantization removes information that larger models can absorb. The 8B parameter count of Ministral provides enough headroom that even 4-bit only costs 2.1pp.

## 4. Speed vs Accuracy Tradeoff

| Model (Quantization) | Exact Match | ms/item | Throughput | Sweet Spot? |
|----------------------|-------------|---------|------------|-------------|
| Llama-3.2-3B (4-bit) | 64.1% | 141.3 | 7.1 items/s | No (too inaccurate) |
| Qwen3-4B (4-bit) | 83.0% | 174.2 | 5.7 items/s | No (too much quant loss) |
| Ministral-8B (4-bit) | 95.8% | 284.3 | 3.5 items/s | Viable backup |
| Ministral-8B (8-bit) | **98.7%** | 344.2 | 2.9 items/s | **Best for dev** |

For interactive voice routing, latency per item is the bottleneck (single inference per user turn). All models are well under the 500ms perceptual threshold. The 344ms Ministral 8-bit latency is imperceptible to users.

## 5. VRAM Footprint Analysis

Target hardware: NVIDIA RTX 4090 (24 GB VRAM)

| Model (Quantization) | Model Size (est.) | GPU Util | VRAM Ceiling | Headroom |
|----------------------|-------------------|----------|--------------|----------|
| Llama-3.2-3B (4-bit) | ~2.0 GB | 0.75 | 18.0 GB | ~16.0 GB |
| Qwen3-4B (4-bit) | ~2.6 GB | 0.75 | 18.0 GB | ~15.4 GB |
| Ministral-8B (4-bit) | ~4.5 GB | 0.75 | 18.0 GB | ~13.5 GB |
| **Ministral-8B (8-bit)** | **~8.7 GB** | **0.85** | **20.4 GB** | **~8 GB** |

The 8-bit Ministral model requires bumping `--gpu_memory_utilization` from 0.75 to 0.85 to accommodate the larger model + KV cache. At 0.85 on 24GB, the ceiling is 20.4GB — leaving ~8GB spare after model weights, KV cache, and embedding tables. This is comfortable for single-request routing inference.

**Note**: The `--quantization gptq_marlin` flag is added to force the fast Marlin GPTQ kernel, per findings in [vLLM Llama-3.2 Latency Analysis](2026.02.11-vllm-llama-3.2-latency-analysis.md).
