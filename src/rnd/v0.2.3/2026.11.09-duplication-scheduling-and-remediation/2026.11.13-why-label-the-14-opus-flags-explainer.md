## What is being decided

The plan sets three bars that Fable already passed on the official proof of 31 October:

| Bar | What it means | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Found | of the 80 known duplicate pairs, how many the judge flags | 0.80 |
| **Correct** | of the pairs the judge flags, how many a person agrees really are duplicates | 0.80 |

If Opus passes all three, the backlog runs on Opus. If it misses one, the backlog waits for Fable.

## Why "correct" depends on labels

"Correct" can only be scored on a flagged pair that a person has labelled. The 222 labelled pairs were chosen months ago, mostly from **Fable's** flags. When Opus flags a pair that Fable did not, nobody has labelled that pair yet.

The proof's rule counts such a pair as **wrong**. That is the strict reading, and it is fair for a pass/fail gate. But it means Opus is marked down for every new pair it finds, even when the pair really is a duplicate.
