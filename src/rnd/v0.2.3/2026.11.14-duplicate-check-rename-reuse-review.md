# Reuse review: the two schema 3 helpers in the duplicate-check history

Clayton's rename added two definitions to `lupin_mcp.duplicate_check_history`: `either` and `upgraded`. The reuse gate at 1809f7a2b found no outcome line for either.
I read the source of both and of the shortlisted neighbours: `line_of`, `read_history`, `read_lines` and the arbiter's `LocalSnapshotStore.get_section`. I also read the five nearest definitions each search named.

The whole tree has one reader of the old names `owed` and `carried`. It is the module's own `LEGACY_NAME` table. `either` and `upgraded` both read that table, so the mapping is kept in one place.
The two helpers do different jobs. `either` reads one count out of a raw result document, and `line_of` calls it. `upgraded` renames the keys of a whole history line, and `read_history` calls it.
`upgraded` cannot be written with `either`. It must drop the old key and must not add a name the line never held, while `either` answers None for a missing count.
