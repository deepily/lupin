## Two-client design, stated once

Each legacy `.js` definition below has a multiplexer `.ts` twin, or the reverse, and both were added by the same commit `c48bfdab0`.
The twin is marked RELATED, not SAME_JOB, on this ground: the legacy client is a classic script that cannot import a TypeScript module, and `4ae37fd96` fixed the rule that the two clients share no code.
The cost is real: the two copies must now be kept in step by hand.
