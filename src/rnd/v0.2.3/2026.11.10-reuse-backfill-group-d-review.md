# Reuse back-fill, group D: legacy-client Background Jobs definitions (row a1653202)

Rick ruled on 10 November 2026 (row 5f8fc615) that older definitions with no outcome line get one. This is group D of six: 52 definitions, 42 in `src/lupin_app/static/js/background-jobs.js` (the legacy client's jobs accordion) and 10 in `src/lupin_app/static/js/multiplexer/render/BackgroundJobsRenderer.ts` (the multiplexer's pane). Looked up on 10 November 2026 against the tree at 574323021.

Every definition in `background-jobs.js` is a copy of a multiplexer function (`multiplexer/jobs/jobsFormat.ts`, `jobsRender.ts`, `BackgroundJobsRenderer.ts`), kept on purpose by Rick's no-shared-code ruling (row 87812328). The symbol index returns no symbol for that file (its functions sit inside one immediately invoked function), so `fetch_similar` answers `UNKNOWN_ENTRY` and no receipt exists. Those 42 carry the `unindexed` token, a ruling line and an outcome line, and no `SEARCHED` line, because that line needs an `rs-` search id. Twins were found by a fixed-string search of `static/js` for each function name, and read side by side for `sortJobs` and `isSafeHref`. The nested `runNow` in the multiplexer renderer is also unknown to the index, so it is handled the same way. The other nine multiplexer definitions have a receipt, a search and a `SEARCHED` line per candidate.
