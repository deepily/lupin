## Phase 2: Voice Settings Enhancement

### 2.1 Enhanced Voice Parameters

```python
"voice_settings": {
    "stability": 0.65,              # Current: 0.5 (test 0.6-0.7)
    "similarity_boost": 0.75,       # Current: 0.5 (test 0.7-0.8)
    "style": 0.3,                   # NEW: Add expressiveness (0.0-1.0)
    "use_speaker_boost": True,      # NEW: Enhance clarity
    "speed": 1.1                    # NEW: Fine-tune rate (0.25-4.0)
}
```

**Parameter Guidelines**:
- **Stability** (0.6-0.7): Higher = more consistent, lower = more expressive
- **Similarity Boost** (0.7-0.8): Higher = closer to original voice
- **Style** (0.2-0.4): Adds emotional expressiveness without overdoing it
- **Speaker Boost**: Improves clarity, especially for phone/compressed audio
- **Speed** (1.0-1.2): Slight speed increase often improves perceived quality
