## 🔴 The finding: `FULL` is red everywhere except the one board where it is green

Computed style, measured rather than eyeballed:

| state | room clause | class | colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| at the line (120%) | `FULL` | `flow-ratio-closed` | `rgb(192,43,43)` **RED** |
| already failing (190%) | `CLOSE 3` | `flow-ratio-closed` | `rgb(192,43,43)` **RED** |
| **idle board (0/0, ratio —)** | **`FULL`** | **`flow-ratio-open`** | **`rgb(27,127,59)` GREEN** |

**The colour tracks ratio-vs-threshold. The room clause tracks room.** Two independent
signals sharing one string, and the idle board is the single reachable state where they
disagree: the word says there is no room, the colour says the gate is open.

⇒ The row anticipated that `FULL` on an empty board might read as **alarming**. It reads as
**reassuring**, which is the worse direction — a board at its stated limit, rendered in the
colour of *you are fine*.

**This is not a bug to patch.** `FULL` on an idle board is Rick's ruling correctly applied
and is pinned by a test; the colour is the gate verdict correctly applied. Both halves are
right and the pair reads wrong. Per the row's own instruction, that makes it a **new
decision for Rick**, not a fix.

## The arm I threw away, because it was a fact about my fixture

My first pass built payloads with `room_for: 0` / `close_needed: 3` but a **ratio below the
gate**. All five states rendered green, which looked like a much larger finding.

Those are inputs the server cannot produce — a board cannot simultaneously be out of room
and comfortably under its threshold. Reporting it would have been a finding about my
fixture rather than about the code. Every figure above uses **coherent** payloads, with the
ratio above the gate whenever room is exhausted.

⇒ Same family as a hand-written fixture being better-formed than reality, reached from the
other side: mine was *worse*-formed than reality, and it manufactured a defect.
