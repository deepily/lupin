## 0. 🔴 First, a retraction — the banked finding named the wrong cause

The finding I inherited, written by my own predecessor at her reap, said:

> *"`memento_io.py verify` lists a seat OK but exits 1 overall on **71 orphan mirrors** repo-wide."*

**The orphans are not why it exits 1, and the fix that sentence implies is already built.**

Read the code: `orphans` is computed, printed under its own banner, and **never appended to
`findings`**. The docstring says so in terms — *"visible always, fatal never"* — and argues the case
better than the finding did:

> *"The mirror is an archive by design… a record that legitimately leaves the repo leaves an orphan
> FOREVER, and the only act that would clear it is deleting the safety copy. A finding whose sole
> remedy is destroying the thing it protects is one people learn to ignore, or worse, obey."*

That reasoning is correct and I am not proposing to touch it. **Had I implemented the banked finding
as written, I would have shipped a no-op and reported it as a fix** — the same shape as the §5.6
defect earlier today: an assertion about a capability nobody re-checked.

**What actually produced the exit 1**, measured:

## 1. The actual defect — there is no seat-level question to ask

`verify` accepts `--repo` and `--show-ok`. **There is no `--persona`.** So a manager at a reap, who
needs to answer *"is this one seat safe to reap"*, has only a 547-file repo-wide verdict and must
grep their own name out of it and eyeball whether any of the 7 findings is theirs.

Measured for my own seat at 17:08:37:

| Rachel 🕊️ | |
|---|---|
| findings (`BARE-SLOT`/`UNMIRRORED`/`DRIFTED`/`MISDIRECTED`) | **0** |
| lines mentioning `rachel` in the output | **15** — every one an `ORPHAN-MIRROR` notice |
| exit code seen by the reaper | **1** |

**Safe to reap, and unprovable without grep.**

He told me he ran three re-spins this session and read that exit 1 three times, reading it as corpus

## 2. What I propose, and what I am NOT proposing

**Add `--persona <name>` to `verify`. Scope the FINDINGS and the EXIT CODE to that seat. Leave the
corpus notices printing exactly as they do now, and leave the orphan policy alone.**

| | repo-wide run (unchanged) | `--persona rachel` |
|---|---|---|
| findings counted toward exit | all 7 | only that seat's — **0** |
| orphan mirrors | 71, listed, notice | still shown, **but attributed**: *"7 of 71 are yours"* |
| question answered | *"is this corpus healthy?"* | *"is this seat safe to reap?"* |

**Exit codes, following the script's own existing doctrine rather than a new one:**

| code | meaning |
|---|---|
| **0** | this seat has no findings — **safe to reap** |
| **1** | this seat has findings — named, with their remedies |
| **4** | 🔴 **this seat was not scanned at all** |

**Exit 4 is the load-bearing one and it is already the script's own idea.** `cmd_verify` today
returns 4 on an empty repo-wide scan set, with the reasoning: *"a scan set of zero has nothing to be
clean about, so reporting it as clean is a lie the old exit code told."* **Per-seat, that lie is one
typo away**: `--persona rachael` must never return 0. A seat-scoped checker that answers "clean" for
a persona it never found is the guard-certifying-itself shape the rewrite exists to prevent — and it
is worse than the bug being fixed, because it fires at a reap.

### What happens to the 71 orphans — asked plainly, answered plainly

**They stay, they stay non-fatal, and nobody is asked to act on them.** Deleting one destroys the
only surviving copy of a record. The docstring's argument holds and I am not reopening it.

**But the notice as it stands is a flat list of 71 paths, and Mr Radio is right that a notice nobody
can act on is the same defect one level down.** The cheap repair is not a remedy — it is
**attribution**, so the notice tells a reader something about themselves:

```
71 orphans by persona (measured 17:08:37):
  mr-radio 12 · clayton 10 · cheech 8 · sam 7 · rachel 7 · krishna 6 · rio 5
  tiberius 4 · tiffany 2 · john 2 · chloe 1 · arnold 1 · 5 rescued/legacy artifacts
```

⚠️ **That is a census, not a verdict**, and the honest form says so: an orphan is *"a record that left
the repo"*, which is normal for an archived seat and alarming for a live one. The script cannot tell
those apart, so it should keep reporting and keep refusing to judge — the same discipline it already
applies to ranking two divergent copies.

## 3. Why a flag rather than "read the output more carefully"

The alternative that costs nothing is telling reapers to grep. It is what happens today, and it
**failed three times this afternoon, to the person who wrote the reap process.** The seat least able
to spot its own line in a 547-file scan is the one mid-reap with a context ceiling. A control that
depends on reading carefully at exactly the moment nobody is reading carefully is not a control.

---

## 4. ⚠️ The argument against this proposal

**It rests on one afternoon and one reader.** Three misreadings, all by Mr Radio, all on the same
day, and he is the same person who commissioned the fix — so the evidence for the defect and the
demand for the remedy come from a single source. A second seat has not independently hit this.

**A cheaper fix exists and I am not certain it loses:** print a per-seat summary line for every
persona that has any finding, keeping one exit code. That fixes the *reading* problem without adding
a flag, an argument surface, or a fourth exit path — and the whole complaint is a reading problem.

**And scoping an exit code is exactly the move that hides things.** This script's history is a
sequence of narrowings that turned out to conceal real damage — `0/0` reading as clean, a mirror
never asked whether the repo still held its record. A `--persona` flag is another narrowing, and the
argument that it is a safe one rests entirely on exit 4 firing when the seat is not found. **If exit
4 is dropped as fussy during implementation, this proposal becomes a new instance of the bug it
describes.** That is the condition on which it should be accepted or refused.

**If one afternoon reads as insufficient**, the honest outcome is to leave this as a research doc and
add the per-seat summary line, which is unarguable and cheap.
