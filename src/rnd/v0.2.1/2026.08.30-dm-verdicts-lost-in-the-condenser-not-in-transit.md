## 6. The third failure Mr. Radio saw is the same component

Maya reported a condensed DM arriving with **an instruction its sender never wrote**
("pause before beginning"). Fabricated directive, fabricated speech act (§2.2) and
inverted retraction (`29a986df`) are three faces of one thing: **the rewriter is free to
change what kind of utterance a clause is.** The existing guards all police *content* —
which facts, which quantities, whose position. **None of them polices the speech act.**

## 7. What I would build, and what I would not

**Not** another content guard — the words were all present in the measured case and it
still misled.

1. **A verdict is its own DM.** Approval, sha, branch, nothing else. Under the trigger by
   construction, so there is nothing to reorder. This is Tiberius's proposal and it is the
   only one of the three that cannot fail quietly.
2. **Never reorder.** Condense in place; a summary may drop a topic but must not promote a
   later one above an earlier one. The measured harm is ordering, not omission.
3. **A speech-act guard** — refuse a rewrite that emits an imperative or interrogative
   label the sender did not write. Narrowest rule that catches §2.2 **and** Maya's
   fabricated directive.

⇒ **(1) is a receipt and (2)–(3) are detectors.** Per the fleet's own doctrine, prefer the
receipt.
