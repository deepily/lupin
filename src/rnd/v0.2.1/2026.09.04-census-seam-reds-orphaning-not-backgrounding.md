## 2. The mechanism, read off the failure rather than inferred

```

src/tests/unit/test_the_census_seam_reads_the_isolated_directory.py:77
    assert count <= len( _LIVE_PIDS )
```

`_LIVE_PIDS = _ancestor_pids( 3 )` runs **at import** and walks parents while `pid > 1`.
Printed from inside the failing launch shape, not deduced:

```
chain_len= 2  pids= [2384366, 1319]  comm chain: ['python', 'systemd']
```

The launcher exited, the process **reparented to the user systemd**, systemd's parent is
pid 1, the walk stops at two, and `2 < 3` trips the assert.

## 3. The reconciliation

**The variable is whether the launching shell OUTLIVES the child**, i.e. whether the
ancestor chain is ≥3 deep at import — not whether the job is backgrounded.

| shape | chain | result |
|---|---|---|
| foreground | 3 | 5 passed |
| `nohup &`, shell stays alive | 3 | 5 passed — Rachel's cell B, and the probe agrees |
| `( nohup … & )`, subshell exits | **2** | **2 failed** |
| harness `run_in_background` | 3 | 5 passed |

⇒ Rachel's zero and john's two are **both correct and about different launch shapes**. Her
`nohup &` kept a live parent, so her chain was 3; his launcher did not survive. **Her
instrument was not broken — it was aimed at a shape that genuinely passes**, which is why
cell B gave 5 passed and why withdrawing the narrowing was right.
