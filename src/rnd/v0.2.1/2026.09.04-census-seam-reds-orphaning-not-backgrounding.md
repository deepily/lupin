ancestor chain is ≥3 deep at import — not whether the job is backgrounded.

| shape | chain | result |
|---|---|---|
| foreground | 3 | 5 passed |
| `nohup &`, shell stays alive | 3 | 5 passed — Rachel's cell B, and the probe agrees |
| `( nohup … & )`, subshell exits | **2** | **2 failed** |

⇒ Rachel's zero and john's two are **both correct and about different launch shapes**. Her
`nohup &` kept a live parent, so her chain was 3; his launcher did not survive. **Her
instrument was not broken — it was aimed at a shape that genuinely passes**, which is why
cell B gave 5 passed and why withdrawing the narrowing was right.
