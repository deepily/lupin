# The live multiplexer drops the epic story row, and the harness does not

## The question

My appearance walk found `epic[epic:alpha]>story[0]` in **legacy only**. john's
cross-client entry found the epic sections **identical, including their story
rows**. María: *"the discriminating question is whether the mux emits
`tr.epic-story-row` on the same fixture, or not — check the fixture first."*

## The fixture, checked first

`accordion-parity-scenario.json` holds **exactly one story**, on `epic:alpha`,
and its own text states its purpose:

> "Alpha exists to prove the story row renders inside the group."

`accordion_stories_body()` wraps it as `{ stories, count: 1 }` — the envelope
shape john established after his own stub returned the bare map.

## Three arms, one fixture, one variable — the venue

| arm | `epic:alpha` rows in DOM | `__drift__` | collapsed | `aria-expanded` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · **LIVE legacy** `/app/notifications?classic=1` | **1** | 1 | true | false |
| 2 · **LIVE mux** `/app/multiplexer` | **0** | 1 | true | false |
| 3 · **HARNESS mux** (john's venue) | **1** | 1 | true | false |

Every other field is identical across all three. Where the row exists it is
`display: none` — collapsed groups build it and hide it, so **absent** and
**present-but-hidden** are separated rather than conflated.

⇒ **Both readings were correct measurements of different venues.** The harness
mux emits the row; the live mux does not.

## The instrument is ruled out, not assumed away

The obvious counter-explanation is that my stub never reached the live mux —
the exact trap john hit twice. It did reach it. Recorded per client:

| client | requested | my stub served it |
|---|---|---|
| LIVE legacy | `/api/epic-stories` | ✅ yes |
| LIVE mux | `/api/epic-stories` | ✅ yes |

**Both clients asked for the endpoint and both received the identical payload.**
The live mux then rendered zero story rows for `epic:alpha`.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Established** — four facts, no arrow between them:

1. the live mux requested and received the stories payload
2. the live mux rendered **0** story rows for `epic:alpha`
3. the harness mux, same fixture, rendered **1**
4. the live legacy, same stub, rendered **1**

🔴 **NOT established: the mechanism.** I did not measure *why* the live mux
drops it — a store parse, an ordering race against the fetch, or a composition
path that never consults the map are all consistent with the four facts above
and I ruled out none of them. **A wrong mechanism sends the next reader into
innocent code**, so there is no mechanism here to follow.

## The part that outlives this row

**The accordion harness does not faithfully represent the live mux for this
row.** That is john's own lesson pointed the other way: his click probe entered
*below* the layer the behaviour lived at and reported the mux inert. Here the
harness enters at a layer that renders **more** than the live page does, and
reports a row the user never sees.

⇒ A harness can diverge from the product in **both** directions, and a
cross-client green taken on it carries that caveat in both.
