## The instrument, and four independent legs

The score is not assumed. Read from the program: `dot_topk( …, metric="cosine" )` in
`src/cosa/rest/db/repositories/vector_search.py`, `similarity_pct = (1 - (col <=> q)) * 100`.
Embeddings are `nomic-embed-text-v1.5`, and **both sides go through `encode_query`** —
`EmbeddingProvider.generate_embedding` never calls `encode_document` outside smoke tests and
warmup — so a stored-vs-stored comparison is the same space as query-vs-stored.

## Why — read from the program, not inferred from the corpus

The score is plain cosine over sentence embeddings, which measure **topic**. Direction ("miles to
km" vs "km to miles"), operand order, and the specific numerals are exactly what determines the
**answer** in this corpus — and they are the tokens a sentence embedding most readily discards.
Inverting a unit conversion changes the answer completely and moves the vector by 1.1 points.

**The single real firing corroborates this.** The one `near_match_confirmed` in the entire trace
corpus was `"What is 17 x 23?"` matching `"What is 17 × 23?"` at 98.37 — an **ASCII-x-versus-×
typographic variant.** The same question written `"what is 17 times 23"` scores **79.54** against that
same `"What is 17 × 23?"` row and does **not** replay — and 79.54 is itself one of the four
real trace records, so this contrast is measured on both sides rather than constructed. ⇒ **At 90.0 the feature is a spelling-variant detector, not a paraphrase
detector.** That is a real and safe function; it is not the one the row asks about.

## A false finding I caught before reporting it

`answer_is_correct` is a **`text`** column holding the literal strings `'true'` and `'null'`, and
`_may_serve` requires `verdict is True`. That looks like a guard that can never pass. **It is not.**
The column is JSON: `answer_is_correct` is in `_JSON_TEXT_COLUMNS` and is hydrated through
`json.loads`, so `'true'` → `True` and `'null'` → `None`. The docstring's claim — *"read the
hydrated object, never the raw column"* — holds. Recorded because a wrong mechanism sends the next
reader into correct code, which is dearer than a wrong number.

## What I did NOT measure

1. **`lupin_db_test`.** Only `lupin_db_dev` was read. A second corpus may look different.
2. **Any traffic-weighted rate.** The 40 labelled pairs are constructed. Nothing here estimates how
   often real users ask a near-paraphrase, so no error *rate* follows from it.
3. **Why `embedding_cache` is in a different vector space.** Observed, not diagnosed.
4. **The deploy date of `8bf71a64`.** Lower bound only, inherited caveat unclosed.
5. **Whether a different signal would separate.** I did not test gist matching, normalized-form
   matching, numeral extraction, or a cross-encoder. The claim here is only that **this** score
   cannot, not that nothing can.
6. **`near_match_declined` / `auto_accepted` / `out_of_range` behaviour.** Zero occurrences
   post-landing; an empty population and a broken path print the same zero.
