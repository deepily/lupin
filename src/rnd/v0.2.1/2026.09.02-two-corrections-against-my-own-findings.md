## 5. 🔴 `git checkout <sha> -- <file>` STAGES, and the obvious undo then reads the INDEX

The sanctioned way to swing between mutation arms — `git stash` being repo-global and unsafe
with live peers — **stages the file**. A later `git checkout -- <file>` restores from the
**index**, not HEAD, silently reinstating the old version.

My control then reported *6 failed / 34 passed*: **indistinguishable from the merge under
test having been lost.**

⇒ Correct undo: `git restore --source=HEAD --staged --worktree <file>`. The technique is
sound; the undo written beside it was wrong, and it fails by quietly restoring a stale file
rather than by erroring.
