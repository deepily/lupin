## THE CRITERION — decided now, before any result exists

Let `fired_at` be the moment the spawn fires, `NAME` the spawner's `session_name`.

**REPRODUCED — tonight's failure is a live defect** *iff both*:
1. a boot receipt appears, dated **after** `fired_at`, under the seat's data root; **and**
2. a watch armed on `NAME` still yields **`DEAD_NO_WAKE`**.

That is the only combination that shows a seat which came back being reported as one that
did not.

**NOT REPRODUCED**, and each of these is a *different* answer, not a fudge:

| outcome | what it means |
|---|---|
| **no receipt appears at all** | the seat genuinely never woke. The alarm would have been **correct**, and tonight's was too. Not a watch defect |
| a receipt appears **and** the checker returns a **non-alarm** verdict | the watch works on this path. Tonight's alarm had a cause **outside** it, still unexplained |
| a receipt appears under a **different data root** than the watch reads | names the **wrong-root** cause specifically |
| a receipt appears whose `tmux_session` **≠ `NAME`** | names the **writer/reader mismatch** cause specifically — the third cause, so far unrealised |

⚠️ **A single fire is a sample of one.** Whatever comes back, it is one spawn under tonight's
conditions. It can demonstrate a defect; it cannot establish absence.
