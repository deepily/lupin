## Document Organization

### Directory Structure

```
research/
├── README.md (this file) ................. Research index
├── synthesis.md .......................... Primary summary document
├── YYYY.MM.DD-{TOPIC_1}.md ............... Individual research paper
├── YYYY.MM.DD-{TOPIC_2}.md ............... Individual research paper
├── YYYY.MM.DD-{TOPIC_3}.md ............... Individual research paper
├── comparisons/ .......................... Technology comparisons
│   ├── {TECH_1}-vs-{TECH_2}.md
│   └── {TECH_3}-evaluation.md
└── assets/ ............................... Supporting materials
    ├── diagrams/
    ├── benchmarks/
    └── references/
```

### Naming Conventions

**Research Papers**: `YYYY.MM.DD-{descriptive-topic-name}.md`
- Example: `2025.09.15-jwt-token-architecture.md`

**Comparison Documents**: `{tech-a}-vs-{tech-b}.md` or `{topic}-comparison.md`
- Example: `sqlite-vs-postgresql.md`

**Synthesis Document**: Always named `synthesis.md`
