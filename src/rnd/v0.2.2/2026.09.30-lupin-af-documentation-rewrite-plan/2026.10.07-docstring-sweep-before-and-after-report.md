# Before and after: the docstring sweep, as scoped

This report covers the package sweep of 2026-10-06, six merged trains. It does not cover all of the source. The sweep's scope left out the test files, the R&D directories, `src/lupin_mcp` and one Docker script. Those are counted below and were not rewritten.

## The result

| Measure | Before, `1419bd54e` | After, `72fb54a2e` |
|---|---|---|
| Docstrings in the swept scope | 7,957 | 7,959 |
| Of those, with a lint finding | 3,406 | 0 |
| Lint findings in the swept scope | 29,838 | 0 |
| Words in the swept scope's docstrings | 655,958 | 591,062 |
| Lint findings in the whole tree | 112,416 | 82,545 |

Every docstring in the swept scope is clean. The swept scope is 859 Python files. Its docstrings lost 64,896 words, about 10%.

The whole tree lost 29,871 findings, about 27% of what it had. The swept scope accounts for 29,838 of them. The other 33 were in 14 docstrings of test files that the trains also touched.

## How it was measured

One linter produced every count: the one in the tree at commit `045d02ad2`, with no uncommitted change under `src/cosa/repo/doc_lint`. Each side of each comparison was read from git, not from the working tree. The run took place on 2026-10-07 at 10:32 EDT.

A docstring is paired with its rewrite by its name and its order among same-named docstrings in the file. A pair counts as changed when the two texts differ.

Two measurements agree. The six trains, each compared with its own parent, sum to 29,871 findings removed and 3,485 docstrings changed. One comparison of the first parent with the last head gives the same 29,871 and the same 3,485.

The plan page gave slightly different figures for trains 1 to 3 and for the morning's tree. Those were taken as each train merged, with the linter of that hour. Train 4 changed the linter's capitals rule. The figures here replace them.

## What was not swept

About 93% of the remaining findings are in test files. The sweep's author gives these reasons for the other two. The docstrings in `src/lupin_mcp` are the tool descriptions an agent reads, so they are prompts. Editing the Docker script invalidates the image's layer cache.

The remaining findings by rule: capitalised words 52,236, sentence length 9,817, summary length 7,601, bare reference 5,346, emphasis glyph 2,788, ISO date 2,023, tic 1,881, preface length 451, dated banner 374, agent imperative 28.

## Limits

- A finding count measures style, not meaning. This report does not show that the rewrites kept what the originals said. The sweep ran its own meaning check on each package. Its totals are not gathered here.
- The pairing rule can mismatch two docstrings when same-named functions are reordered in a file. That was not checked.
- The test results for the final head are the sweep author's and are not repeated here.
- A second edition follows if the test files are swept.
