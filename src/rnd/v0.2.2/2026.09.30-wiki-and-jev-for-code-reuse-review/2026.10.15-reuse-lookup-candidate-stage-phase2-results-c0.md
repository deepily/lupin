## How this was produced

- Code: `src/cosa/repo/symindex/candidates/arm_run.py` at the `code_sha` above, run as
  `python -m cosa.repo.symindex.candidates.arm_run --data <reuse-review> --needs <in-sample needs> --fresh-sha <hash> --manifest <baseline manifest> --out <page> --code-sha <sha>`.
- Needs: the 30 in-sample needs, `io/tmp/2026.10.10-clayton-flip-needs-v2.json`. The 30 reserved needs were not read. Their file's hash was passed to the guard, which refuses on a match.
- Reference: the shortlist of the full-sweep verdict for the new question, from the cached answers, as cut by the verdict rule to at most ten ids.
- Fusion constants: the defaults (k 60, depth 512, head 5). They are not tuned yet, because the vector arms are not measured.
- The need's own entry is excluded from the cached answers but still takes a shortlist slot, which costs recall slightly at small K.
- The control row of the verdict replay is the full sweep. Its 20% verdict flip rate is the judge's own noise on the whole set, so it is a reference line and not an arm: an arm at 10% is not "better than" it.
- The rank-1 count depends on the order BM25 gives to tied scores, which is the index order of the snapshot. A different snapshot can move it by a few members.
- The twin table is not comparable to the judge's 337 of 376 first-place figure, which ranks by the judge's scores and this ranks by BM25.
