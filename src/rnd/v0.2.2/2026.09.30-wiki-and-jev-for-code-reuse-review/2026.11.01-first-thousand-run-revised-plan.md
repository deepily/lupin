# The rest of the first-thousand trial: a revised plan

*Lupin reuse project, 1 November 2026. A proposal from Cheech for Rick after reading the expert's response. Nothing here has been built or run.*

## 1. What the response changes, in one line

The trial spent its budget hunting for a second piece of code that does the same job as each seed. A test item may not need that second piece at all, because the seed itself already answers the need it was written from.

## 2. What I checked before writing this

- **The expert's question "why only 8 drafts from 36 seeds?" has a simple answer.** The program only writes a need for a seed after it has found a partner: some other function confirmed as doing the same job. 33 of the 36 seeds found no partner, so they were dropped before any need was written (the code counts them as `no_partner`). The 8 drafts all came from the 3 seeds that did find one.
- **So the partner requirement is the cost driver.** 673 of the 686 calls went into searching for partners.
- **What the saved records can and cannot tell us without new calls:** every one of the 673 yes/no rulings is stored, along with its seed, candidate and route. The reasons the 6 drafts were rejected for leaking the answer are **not** stored, so that question needs a code change before it can be answered.

## 9. Expert 1: where the data agrees, and where it corrects him

**Agrees.** The control is not matrix-matched. Option E, the production writer on the plants, is the right fix, and A, B, C and D are worse for the reasons he gives. Score retrieval and judge separately. Report the undrafted pairs in an intention-to-treat count instead of dropping them. Replace the all-or-nothing stop with pre-registered marks.

**Corrects.**

1. *"The prose list gap is the smaller half; the vector text is probably the larger."* The vector text is nearly the whole effect (10 to 1 of 17), and the prose list on its own barely moves the plants (5 to 6). Both come from the same missing line, so E fixes both at once.
2. *Tier 0 calibrated at 98% recall, halt on 2 misses of 20.* Our known-item recall under leak-checked needs is about 72%: 12 of 17 control originals, 27 of 37 attribution needs. His halt rule would fire today on the originals, with no plant involved. The pass mark therefore has to be **paired**: the plant against its own original, on the same need.
3. *All twenty pairs are work-alikes.* Five are not duplicates by the human labels. A positive control must not count a miss on them against the checker. They become a negative control instead: a find there is a false positive.
4. *About one judge call per need.* The judge reads 128 candidates, 50 per call, so about three calls per need. His per-step figures roughly triple.
5. *The 17 of 20 dry run as a lexical ceiling.* Agreed, and Chloé already showed its stand-ins carried the forbidden names.

What stands behind all of this is a product finding, not a control finding: when a need is written without the code's own words, the search misses the exact item about 28% of the time, and the judge almost never does. The control's 5 of 17 sat on top of that.

## 10. Pre-registered scoring for the rerun (run -m)

These rules are fixed before the run and do not move after it. Revised 20:45 EST after Chloé's design review (`io/write-ups/2026.11.01-section-10-scoring-holes-chloe.md`, nine holes, all taken).

- **Population, pinned.** The label rule is `label_load.resolved_labels` with the third rater: where labels-rick (sha256 `e87e9ef367406fce`) and labels-reviewer-2 (`947b20b8af234309`) agree, that label; where they differ, labels-jason's label (`2d7e4033a0263235`; he labelled all six disputed pairs of this stratum, none unsure, so no ruling by Rick is needed). Key: answer-key.json `f17236f7cf913fca`, planted.json `4ec34350a03203c4`. **Not the same job (5)**: d-35e19468ee, d-5270a48d23, d-e4d0d1e147, d-7a334b0576, d-803557386f. **Same job (15)**: the other fifteen. The report reads this list as a committed table and never re-derives it. Primary figures are over the same-job pairs; the five others are the negative control.
- **Three figures per pass**, each with a Wilson interval: retrieval recall (plant in the 128), judge recall given it was in the 128, and end to end. On frozen drafts retrieval is deterministic, so the retrieval figure is **one sample**, the same in both passes; only the judge varies between passes, and draft variance is not sampled at all. The report says so beside the figures.
- **Intention to treat.** Found of 20 with every undrafted pair counted as a miss, and the undrafted list with each pair's reason and word history, as the report already carries it. A plant whose purpose line the screen refused is not planted bare: it counts as a miss and is listed.
- **How the original's figure is measured** (settled 21:35 EST after Chloé's review of John's first sha): the control was written to leave the original out of its own search, but the id it passes as the exclusion is the pair's `d-` id and no index entry carries one, so nothing has ever been excluded. The original stays in the pool and competes with the plant: **one search, two ranks per pair**, which is the cleaner paired measurement, and it is now the rule. John's sha pins it with a guard that raises if the exclusion id ever matches an index id, and corrects the wiki sentence that said the original was left out. The replay's "originals 12 of 17" and the live check's figures are therefore the same measurement.
- **Pass mark, paired, on discordant pairs.** Over the same-job pairs that drafted, count **a** = original in the 128 and plant not, and **b** = plant in the 128 and original not. The control passes when **a − b ≤ 2**, read as "the plants are not worse than the originals by more than two pairs", never as "equivalent". The margin of two is an allowance for a structural effect, not a measured tolerance: the need is written from the original, so its words converge with the original's production line more than with the plant's. On tonight's drafts (pass 1, ledger `18994ca36cc3f33f`; pass 2, `45297a97e24560cd`) the 13 same-job pairs that drafted give a = 6, b = 2, both found 3, neither 2; the mark is re-derived on run -m's own drafted set.
- **No halt.** No plant miss halts the run. The originals' own rate is reported with its Wilson interval beside the attribution stage's 27 of 37; it gates nothing, because at n of about 13 a halt line fires on noise about one time in nine.
- **Negative control: report only, no mark.** For the five not-same pairs: how many were retrieved, how many reached the judge (the denominator), and how many the judge accepted. None of the five has reached the judge yet.
- **With and without the unsure-flagged pairs.** Every primary figure is given twice: over all same-job pairs, and leaving out the pairs whose label note starts with the unsure marker.
- **Leakage audit.** Word overlap between each need and its plant's generated line, beside the same overlap with the original's production line. Reported, not gated.
- **Before and after.** Each plant's vector and fused rank with and without its purpose line (expert 1's check H), free from the two runs; the plant's `file` field is unchanged between them, so only the line differs.

#### 2. The figure, per comparison

Unit: the pair. **D = (same-job plants accepted ÷ 13) − (not-same plants accepted ÷ 4)**, per arm and per repeat. With three repeats the figures are:

- **D̄(arm)** = the mean of D over the arm's three repeats, and its **spread** = the largest D minus the smallest D over those repeats.

#### 3. What counts as moved (per comparison)

All five must hold, else "did not move":

1. **Size:** Δ ≥ **+0.40**.
2. **Every repeat agrees:** the lowest D of the later arm's three repeats is above the highest D of the earlier arm's three. Repeat numbers carry no meaning, so the repeats are not paired by number.
3. **More than one pair:** the net count of pairs moving toward separation, by pair status, is at least 2 (not-same accepted→rejected or same-job rejected→accepted +1; the reverse −1).
4. **Not bought by rating everything lower:** same-job pairs accepted (by status) in the later arm are at most 1 below the earlier arm.
5. **Above the noise floor:** Δ is larger than **arm 0's spread** in D over its three repeats, the floor for all three comparisons. If arm 0's spread is itself 0.40 or more, no comparison can be labelled moved and the read says "noise-dominated". Each arm's own spread is printed; a comparison where the earlier or later arm's spread is larger than arm 0's carries that figure in its line.

Labels per comparison, exclusive, in order: **worse** (the reverse of 1 and 2: Δ ≤ −0.40 and the highest later D below the lowest earlier D) · **moved** (1 to 5 hold) · **within noise**.
