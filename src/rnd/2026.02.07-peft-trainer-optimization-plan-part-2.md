## Executive Summary

Phase 2 training completed successfully, achieving **99.0% pre-quantization** and **95.6% post-quantization** exact match accuracy across 32 commands. However, three problems remain:

1. **No consolidated comparison view** — Validation results from pre-training, post-training, and post-quantization stages are printed independently and discarded; manual comparison was required to compare results
2. **No explicit agent routing data** — Users cannot say "Switch me to the math agent" or "Connect me with the weather specialist" — only task-specific phrases are trained
3. **Quantization degradation** — 6 of 32 commands degrade after 4-bit quantization, with podcast generator (-33.3pp) and math (-26.7pp) being the worst

This document defines three workstreams to address these problems, plus a sample size increase from 1200 → 1500 samples/command.

## Part C: Strengthen Quantization-Degraded Commands

### Goal

Review the commands most degraded by 4-bit quantization, analyze their training data for parseability weaknesses, and supplement with stronger disambiguating examples that survive precision loss.

### Quantization Degradation Ranking (Pre-Quant → Post-Quant)

| Command | Pre-Quant | Post-Quant | Delta | Priority |
|---------|-----------|------------|-------|----------|
| podcast generator | 100% | 66.67% | -33.3pp | **HIGH** |
| math | 100% | 73.33% | -26.7pp | **HIGH** |
| todo list | 100% | 80.00% | -20.0pp | **MEDIUM** |
| none | 86.67% | 73.33% | -13.3pp | **MEDIUM** |
| calendar | 100% | 93.33% | -6.7pp | LOW |
| receptionist | 100% | 93.33% | -6.7pp | LOW |

### Analysis

**podcast generator** (-33.3pp): Likely confused with deep research or research-to-podcast after quantization. Templates need stronger podcast/audio-specific anchors that survive precision loss. Current file has only 65 templates.

**math** (-26.7pp): Likely confused with "none" or date-and-time. Math templates include many direct questions that could be general knowledge. Need stronger framing like "calculate", "solve", "do the math on".

**todo list** (-20.0pp): May be confused with calendar (both involve scheduling/tasks). Need strong todo-specific anchors: "add to my list", "check off", "mark as done".

**none** (-13.3pp): General catch-all needs more diverse negative examples to resist quantization drift.

### Approach

**Step 1: Supplement HIGH priority commands**

**podcast generator** (File: `synthetic-data-agent-routing-podcast-generator.txt`):
- Add ~15-20 templates with unambiguous podcast intent
- Every template has at least one strong anchor: "podcast", "episode", "audio show", "listen"
- Examples: "create an audio podcast about...", "generate a podcast episode on...", "make me a podcast discussing..."

**math** (File: `synthetic-data-agent-routing-math.txt`):
- Add ~15-20 templates with explicit math framing
- Strong anchors: "calculate", "solve", "equation", "do the math"
- Examples: "calculate this for me:", "solve this equation:", "what's the math on..."

**Step 2: Supplement MEDIUM priority commands**

**todo list** (File: `synthetic-data-agent-routing-todo-lists.txt`):
- Add ~10-15 templates with strong todo-specific anchors
- Anchors: "my list", "check off", "mark as done", "add a task"

**none** (File: `synthetic-data-none-of-the-above.txt`):
- Add ~10-15 templates in underrepresented categories
- Focus on general knowledge questions that should NOT route to any agent

### Quality Check

Each proposed supplement template must be checked against other command templates to ensure no ambiguity. Quality over quantity — a template that confuses two commands is worse than no template at all.

## Sample Size Increase: 1200 → 1500

### Rationale

- Phase 2 full training completed in under 2 hours — hardware has headroom
- Adding ~150 new templates across Parts B and C increases raw training variety
- 1500/command × 33 commands = ~49,500 pre-split samples (up from ~38,400)
- More examples help quantized model retain precision on boundary cases

### Implementation

File: `src/scripts/run-agentic-intent-training.sh` — change `sample_size_per_command=1200` to `sample_size_per_command=1500` in the `generate` mode block.
