| # | pattern | example | literal grep sees it? |

## 5. ⚠️ WHY THE PER-PANE FIGURES ARE NOT COMPARABLE TO THE PRIOR

His: epic 14 · task 20 · holding 12 · fleet 4. Mine: epic 34 · task 21 · holding 34 · **fleet 4**.

**`fleet` is the only pane that matches, and it is the only pane with no shared classes.** That is
the tell. The task-row machinery (`task-col-*`, `task-disclosed-*`, `task-controls-row`, …) is
rendered into **all three** of task, holding and epic — deliberately, per spec Trap 2, because the
JS card shares `_renderRow` and the TS side must reproduce cell-for-cell row identity.

I count a shared class **once per pane it appears in**; the prior appears to attribute each class to
**one** pane. Neither is wrong; they answer different questions. **Mine answers "what must be styled
for THIS pane to look right", which is the question S3b needs.** Column sums therefore exceed the
population total by construction.
