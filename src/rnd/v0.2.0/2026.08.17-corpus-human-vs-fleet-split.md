## Finding

**This converts the filter question into a SCOPE question** — "should the study run on fleet traffic at all?" — which is **Rick's call, not a fix María can make in the plan.** There is no human-vs-fleet ratio to tune; there is only fleet.

## What was counted / what was NOT

- **Counted:** every row of the FULL corpus, classified by the `from` field.
- **NOT the field the task named:** the corpus jsonl has **no `sender_persona` key**. Its actual keys are `body, chars, directness, from, from_project, from_session, len_grade, overall, sentences, to, to_session, tone, ts, words`. `sender_persona` is the DM *API request* model's field (`routers/dm.py`), not the corpus schema. I measured `from`, the real sender field — verified from the data, not assumed.
- **NOT the fired replay set:** this is the whole corpus (7,317+), not the ~995-and-growing tutor-fired subset (§1.1). The fired subset is still all-fleet by construction, but its per-persona percentages will differ slightly.
- **No message bodies were read or recorded** — persona counts only.
