## 1. Where the submitted half lives — the row's step one

`notifications` holds delivered bodies only, as the row says. The submitted half is kept
somewhere else entirely:

```
/mnt/DATA01/include/www.deepily.ai/projects-data/lupin/dm-corpus/dm_traffic.jsonl
15,298 rows · 31.6 MB · 2026-08-02T20:28 → 2026-08-29T22:28
```

Written by `_append_dm_traffic_row` in `src/cosa/rest/routers/dm.py`, which keeps both halves
deliberately — `body` is what the sender submitted, `delivered_body` what arrived, with
`body_was_rewritten` already computed. Its own comment says why: *"with only the delivered text
you cannot recover what the sender wrote, and with only the submitted text you cannot see what
was sent."*

⇒ **The feared finding does not hold.** The condenser IS auditable. The corpus was simply not
where the search went.

## 5. The inversion shape is NOT measurable this way — and that is the finding

**D2** was written twice and neither version is trustworthy:

| version | hits | rate | why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| loose — dropped result numeral **+** delivered asserts failure | 135 | 15.14% | **Artefact.** Sampling shows honest rows: `"4 failed / 8844 passed"` delivered with counts intact and an unrelated numeral dropped while condensing. |
| tight — the above **+** submitted does not assert failure | 18 | 2.12% | **Misses its own positive control.** Control 2 is not flagged. |

The tight version fails for a reason worth keeping: **a refutation quotes what it refutes.**
Tiberius's message contains `not exit` and `ERROR` because he was *rebutting* a failure claim.
"The sender used no failure words" is not a proxy for "the sender reported no failure".

⇒ By this row's own criterion — *"a census method that misses them is measuring something
else"* — **2.12% is disqualified, and so is 15.14%.** Reporting either as the rate would hand
Rick a number with no evidence under it. I am reporting neither.

## 6. What this means for the guard decision

This corroborates, from a different direction, the split I filed on `de03a059`:

- **Ratio re-binding** is surface-checkable. It is also **rare — 1 in 898 over four weeks.** A
  guard is cheap to specify; whether it is worth a gate that can reject real messages, at that
  rate, is Rick's call and now has a denominator under it.
- **Verdict inversion** resisted three surface formulations. That is empirical support for the
  worry that it sits outside where `gate` is allowed to reach — it draws its line at
  "structurally checkable" and leaves meaning-reversal to the human reader.

## What I did not establish

- **A rate for the inversion shape.** Not for lack of trying — three formulations, none
  passing its own control. It likely needs the delivered claim compared against the submitted
  *claim* rather than against tokens, which is a different instrument.
- Whether the 135 loose hits contain real corruptions among the false ones. I sampled, I did
  not adjudicate all 135. **A sample is not a census** — if the inversion rate matters, that
  adjudication is the next piece of work.
