⇒ **The feared finding does not hold.** The condenser IS auditable. The corpus was simply not

## 5. The inversion shape is NOT measurable this way — and that is the finding

**D2** was written twice and neither version is trustworthy:

| version | hits | rate | why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| loose — dropped result numeral **+** delivered asserts failure | 135 | 15.14% | **Artefact.** Sampling shows honest rows: `"4 failed / 8844 passed"` delivered with counts intact and an unrelated numeral dropped while condensing. |
| tight — the above **+** submitted does not assert failure | 18 | 2.12% | **Misses its own positive control.** Control 2 is not flagged. |

The tight version fails for a reason worth keeping: **a refutation quotes what it refutes.**
Tiberius's message contains `not exit` and `ERROR` because he was *rebutting* a failure claim.
"The sender used no failure words" is not a proxy for "the sender reported no failure".

⇒ By this row's own criterion — *"a census method that misses them is measuring something
else"* — **2.12% is disqualified, and so is 15.14%.** Reporting either as the rate would hand
Rick a number with no evidence under it. I am reporting neither.

## 6. What this means for the guard decision

This corroborates, from a different direction, the split I filed on `de03a059`:

- **Ratio re-binding** is surface-checkable. It is also **rare — 1 in 898 over four weeks.** A
  guard is cheap to specify; whether it is worth a gate that can reject real messages, at that
  rate, is Rick's call and now has a denominator under it.
- **Verdict inversion** resisted three surface formulations. That is empirical support for the
  worry that it sits outside where `gate` is allowed to reach — it draws its line at
  "structurally checkable" and leaves meaning-reversal to the human reader.

## What I did not establish

- **A rate for the inversion shape.** Not for lack of trying — three formulations, none
  passing its own control. It likely needs the delivered claim compared against the submitted
  *claim* rather than against tokens, which is a different instrument.
- Whether the 135 loose hits contain real corruptions among the false ones. I sampled, I did
  not adjudicate all 135. **A sample is not a census** — if the inversion rate matters, that
  adjudication is the next piece of work.

# Addendum — the 0.11% was a FLOOR, and here is the gap that made it one

**Row** `7fb0db55` · **Rio** · **2026-08-29**, a few hours after the census above.

Mr Radio ran his own cruder pass over the same corpus and found a **third real specimen** that
my census never counted. He asked one question and would not accept a guess at it: that
specimen is a test result, so was it inside my 898 and missed by the detector, or outside the
denominator altogether? The two answers mean different things.

## The specimen

```
SENT       "...which is the RED commit: the test lands there, the alias is still present
            there by design, so 3 fail and 2 pass."
DELIVERED  "At 78f6683d, the test fails 3 out of 5 times, which is expected..."
```

Three of five **tests** failing became one test failing three of five **times**. Both counts
survive and the ratio is arithmetically true, so no count check can see it — but a
deterministic, explained, by-design red has been re-described as a **flaky** one. Flaky and
deterministic call for opposite responses: a flaky red gets re-run, and re-running this one
would have failed again and confirmed the wrong diagnosis.

## The answer: **B**, and the detector was never the problem

Run against the real corpus row (`2026-08-25T02:21:47`, `live`, `body_was_rewritten`):

| call | result |
|---|---|
| `carries_a_result( submitted )` | **False** — the row never entered the 898 |
| `fabricated_ratio( submitted, delivered )` | **`[('3','5')]`** — D1 would have flagged it |

**The gate in front of the detector was too narrow; the detector itself was correct.** That
distinction matters for what to fix — nothing was wrong with how corruption is recognised.

The gap is one line. `OUTCOME` listed inflected forms only — `passed|passing|failed|failing|
failure` — and **not the bare present tense**. A sender writing *"so 3 fail and 2 pass"*
stated a result the gate could not see.

## What the gap cost, measured

| | shipped | fixed |
|---|---|---|
| denominator | 898 | **977** (+79, 8.1% of the fixed denominator) |
| D1 hits | 1 | **2** |
| D1 rate | 0.11% | **0.20%** |

**The rate doubled on a one-line vocabulary fix, so treat 0.20% the same way — as a floor.**
The honest claim is not "the rate is 0.20%"; it is "0.20% is what *this* vocabulary can see."

## Why bare `pass`/`fail` is admitted in the LEADING form only

A first cut admitted the trailing form too and pushed the denominator to 1,011. Sampling the
newly-admitted rows found the reason to refuse it: **trailing bare "pass 3" is an ordinal** —
a review pass, a second sweep — not a result. Roughly one newly-admitted row in seven was that
shape, which inflates the denominator and deflates the rate. The inflected forms keep their
trailing form, where `passed 16861` and `green 20/20` live; a stricter cut that dropped the
trailing form wholesale lost 17 real rows, so it was rejected too.

## What moved, and what deliberately did not

- Specimen 3 is now **positive control 3** in the script. All three controls sit in the
  denominator; controls 1 and 3 are flagged, control 2 is the inversion shape and still is not.
- The script printed a **union rate** mixing D1 with the disqualified D2. It now prints D1's
  rate and says in words that D2 has none. A union rate launders a disqualified detector into
  a publishable figure.
- **D2's count moved 18 → 20** with the widened vocabulary. That is noise on a disqualified
  instrument, reported rather than hidden: it still misses its own positive control, so it
  still gets no rate.
- **Nothing about the condenser changed.** No guard was built. That decision is Rick's and it
  waits on these numbers.

## A fourth mechanism, and it is invisible to every count check

Four of the five — 01, 09, 10, 17 — share a shape the existing three do not cover:

> **DISPOSITION FLIP.** The delivered text reverses the sender's disposition of a thing —
> works/broken, deleted-on-purpose/failed, still-open/already-fixed, right-answer/wrong-field —
> while every numeral is either absent or intact and correctly bound.

The three known mechanisms all leave a numeric fingerprint: a re-bound ratio, a dropped operand,
a count re-scoped from tests to runs. **This one leaves none.** No count check, no ratio check
and no numeral-difference check can see it, which is exactly why D2 — a detector keyed on a
*dropped numeral* — could not measure it. D2 found these five by accident: in four of them the
numeral it flagged is not the thing that broke.

The fifth (18) is mechanism 1 repeating, which is worth knowing on its own: quantity re-binding
is no longer a single specimen.

## What a rate would need — named, not built

Comparing tokens cannot reach a disposition flip; the numerals agree in all four. A rate would
need the **delivered claim compared against the submitted claim** — a claim-level judge
(structured claim extraction, or an LLM adjudicator scored against these five as controls), not
a regex. That is a different instrument and this row did not build it.

## The twelve honest ones, and one thing that is NOT a finding
