# Self-re-spin E2E: observing a disposable seat from outside — plan (design only)

**Row:** 9e0678f6 · **Author:** Arnold 🪨 (Tester, Cheech's crew) · **Status:** plan only, NO firing until the verb lands and Cheech spawns the seat.

## The rule this plan obeys
Never fire a self-clear at a working fleet seat. The E2E runs against a **disposable seat** Cheech spawns for the purpose — a throwaway persona/session, seeded with a memento so `/clear` rehydrates it. The observer is **read-only** and lives **outside** the seat, because the seat that clears cannot report its own outcome.

## The oracle is already shipped
`cosa.agents.heartbeat_arbiter.self_respin_observer.observe_fleet_self_respin` is the acceptance instrument. It globs the on-disk markers under `fleet_data_root()`, matches each to the live context-pressure record by `session_id`, and returns a `SelfRespinAssessment` per marker. Its verdicts ARE the pass/fail:

| Verdict | is_alarm | Meaning for the run |
|---|---|---|
| `RETURNED` | False | **SUCCESS** — same seat (session_id + tmux_session), `over_budget → within_budget`, fresh turn dated after `fired_at`. |
| `DEAD_NO_RETURN` | True | **FAILURE — did not come back.** Past `expected_return_by` with no confirmed return. |
| `IDENTITY_MISMATCH` | True | **FAILURE** — a different seat answered under this marker's session_id. |
| `PENDING` | False | In flight, inside the window — keep polling, not yet a verdict. |

## Procedure (when the verb lands)
1. **Spawn the disposable seat** (Cheech) — throwaway persona, seeded memento, confirm `persona_state: allocated`. Record its `session_id` + `tmux_session` from the bridge.
2. **Pre-snapshot (outside)** — read the seat's context-pressure record; capture identity tuple + `status`. **Never forge `pre_clear_status`.** A real firing needs `pre_clear_status == "over_budget"`; if we cannot cheaply drive the disposable seat genuinely over the line, the run does NOT fabricate the field — it reports exactly what it proved (same seat returned at low context after a real clear) and states plainly that the **over-budget trigger was NOT exercised**. A missing claim is honest; a forged high side is a green that reads as proof forever.
3. **Fire** — the seat calls `self_respin()`: it writes the marker (via `build_marker_dict`) under `fleet_data_root()` **before** scheduling `/clear`, then the detached injection types `/clear` (proven to outlive its caller — `test_detached_injection_outlives_caller.py`).
4. **Observe from outside** — poll `observe_fleet_self_respin(now=…)` from the arbiter or a peer, on a cadence, until a terminal verdict or the deadline:
   - `RETURNED` → SUCCESS, stop.
   - `DEAD_NO_RETURN` / `IDENTITY_MISMATCH` → FAILURE, stop, alarm.
   - `PENDING` → keep polling until `now >= expected_return_by`, then the classifier itself flips it to `DEAD_NO_RETURN`.
5. **Teardown** — reap the disposable seat; delete its marker file.

## What result means "it did NOT come back" (the failure signal)
A returned `SelfRespinAssessment` with **`is_alarm == True`** for the disposable seat's session_id — concretely `DEAD_NO_RETURN` (past `expected_return_by`, the session never reappears at `within_budget` with a fresh turn) or `IDENTITY_MISMATCH` (a foreign seat answered). Equivalently: the context-pressure payload never shows that `session_id` transitioning `over_budget → within_budget` with `last_turn_age_s` placing its last turn at/after `fired_at`, before the deadline. Silence past the deadline is failure, not "still waiting" — that is exactly the silently-dead-seat the whole design guards against.

## Why this is safe
- Disposable seat only; no working seat is ever cleared.
- The observer performs no writes and no injections — it reads markers + the pressure payload.
- The `/clear` reaches only the disposable pane (the verb targets the seat's own session_id, and the E2E's seat IS the disposable one).
- The negative outcome is a first-class verdict, so a seat that dies is reported, never assumed alive.
