## Cheech — what you're being asked to do

Rick wants to **play** the Twisty Tunnel Maze (a "twisty little passages, all alike" CLI adventure),
but have **you** run the job process on his behalf. Your session drives the game; **Rick sees the
room narration and answers the "which passage?" prompts inside his own notifications conversation**,
and his answers stream back to your running process.

## The one thing that makes this work: the job's owner email

Every notification and blocking prompt a job emits is delivered to a single recipient decided by the
job's **`user_email`** (→ `get_user_by_email` → that user's WebSocket sessions). So:

- **Construct/launch the maze job with `user_email = <Rick's registered email>`.** That — and only
  that — makes the game surface in Rick's client.
- **Your persona ("Cheech") is a display label only. It does NOT route anything.** Rick is not
  "inheriting your identity"; the job is simply *owned by Rick*. Do not expect persona or DM tricks
  to deliver the game to him — DM and persona channels are same-user-scoped and cannot cross accounts.

## Do this

1. Confirm **Rick is a registered user and currently connected** to his notifications client
   (otherwise prompts queue or return 503).
2. Launch the maze via the **CLI / programmatic job path**
   (e.g. `python -m cosa.agents.twisty_maze.cli`), passing **Rick's email as the job owner**
   (`--user-email <rick>` or the equivalent job-construction parameter). The job's `_execute()`
   stamps `cosa_interface.TARGET_USER = self.user_email`, so all `notify()` / `ask_*` route to Rick.
3. Let it run. Rick's job card ("Twisty Tunnel Maze Game") shows each room in place (via
   `notify(queue_name="run", job_id=…, progress_group_id=…)`); each turn asks Rick "which passage?"
   via `ask_multiple_choice`; his answer advances the deterministic engine in your process.
4. For a dry, LLM-free walkthrough (demo rehearsal), invoke the job's `_execute_dry_run()` path.

## Do NOT

- **Do NOT use the raw cosa-voice MCP `notify` / `ask_*` from your own session** to talk to Rick —
  those ignore persona and go to a single host-configured recipient, not to Rick. Route through the
  **job's** `voice_io` / `cosa_interface` path (which stamps `target_user`).
- **Do NOT launch via the authenticated web submit endpoints** (e.g. `/api/twisty-maze/submit`) for
  this Cheech-runs-for-Rick case — they hard-set the job owner to the *caller* (you), with no
  on-behalf-of override, so the game would land in *your* client, not Rick's. Use the
  CLI / programmatic path where `user_email` is a free parameter.

## How you'll know it's working

Room text appears on **Rick's** notifications screen (not yours) under the "Twisty Tunnel Maze Game"
job card; a "which passage?" question appears for **Rick** to answer; his selection returns to your
process and the next room renders. If nothing appears for Rick, first check that his email is the job
owner and that he's connected.
