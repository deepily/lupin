## Note A — `speech.py:338` is determined, not undetermined

The plan records this as *"push_job with 1 of 4 args; broken or dead; undetermined."* It is
**determined and it is broken**:

- `push_job( self, question, websocket_id, user_id, user_email )` — four required parameters
  (`rest/todo_fifo_queue.py:527`).
- The call passes one: `todo_queue.push_job( munger.transcription )` (`routers/speech.py:338`).
- It therefore raises `TypeError` every time it is reached. It is reached whenever
  `munger.is_agent()` is true, i.e. `self.mode == trans_mode_vox_cmd_agent`
  (`rest/multimodal_munger.py:1249`).

It is also **not an internal caller** — it sits inside the live HTTP handler
`upload_and_transcribe_mp3_file` at `routers/speech.py:216`. Listed as door 8 above.

## Note B — two call sites, one endpoint

`mock_job.py:346` is inside the helper `_handle_expeditor_test(...)`, reached from the same
`POST /api/mock-job/submit` handler as `:192`. `podcast_generator.py:540` and `:729` are two
branches of the single handler `submit_podcast_job`. Counted once each as endpoints, twice each as
call sites — hence 14 raw-push sites across 12 endpoints.

## Note C — doors 6 and 7, confirmed

Both reconstruct a job through `resume_job(...)` and call `todo_queue.push( job )` directly. No
routing, no cache, no confirmation, and **none of `push_job`'s four parameters** (`question`,
`websocket_id`, `user_id`, `user_email`) is involved. The plan's warning — that a read guard
installed at the head of `push_job` does not cover them — is correct, and now extends to the ten
further raw-push endpoints in rows 9–18 and the six internal callers i2–i7.

## Note D — what the shape of the list implies for the scope

The three mechanisms are not three styles of the same thing. `push_job` **asks a question**;
`push_job_agentic` **submits a named command with args**; raw `push` **hands over an already-built
job object**. Sixteen of the eighteen doors are one of the latter two. A front door built around
"route a question" is the right shape for door 1 and questionable for the other seventeen — which is
the design fork the plan flags at owed-item 3, arriving with a much larger denominator than the
table gave it.
