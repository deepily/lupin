# Arm 4 Phase 2 — live measurement, n=40 stratified

**Date**: 2026-08-07
**By**: María 🌸 — independent review run, not the build
**Code under test**: `229810d7` (before the 5% minimum-gain gate landed in `4b8941cd`)
**Corpus**: pinned by [`CORPUS-MANIFEST.md`](CORPUS-MANIFEST.md) — 2,951 records, full sha256 recorded there. The corpus itself is deliberately **not** in git: it is 4 MB of real DM bodies carrying an email address, unreleased defects and named colleagues, and redacting it well enough to publish would have to match the freeze extractor's recall, which over-freezes on purpose. The manifest makes the run auditable without disclosing the traffic.
*(This header originally cited only `sha256_16 94b1c192bf777e03`. A 16-char prefix catches drift; it is not a pin. The manifest is the pin.)*
**Raw rows**: `src/tmp/arm4/maria_live_rows_n40_2026.08.07.json`
**Sample**: 10 per length band, deterministic stride, no RNG

**Verdict: the mechanism is safe and correct. The yield is ~22% of what the plan projected, and the reason inverts one of the plan's core assumptions.**

---

## 1. Compression degrades as messages grow

| Band | delivered n | mean gain | plan target | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<80` words | 5 | **17.8%** | 15% | ✅ meets |
| `80–150` | 5 | 23.1% | 30% | short 7 pt |
| `150–250` | 5 | 11.2% | 45% | short 34 pt |
| `250+` | 5 | 5.7% | 60% | **short 54 pt** |

The plan's §2 economics are built on **size-scaled** ratios — 15% on the smallest messages rising to 65% on the largest, because that is where the tokens are. **Phi-4 does the opposite**: it compresses the cheap messages well and the expensive ones barely at all.

This is not a tuning miss. It is the wrong shape.

## 2. What that costs, in the only units that matter

Applying the 5% minimum-gain gate post-hoc, weighted by real corpus token mass per band:

| | tokens/day |
|---|---|
| Plan projection (§2) | **30,522** |
| **Measured** | **6,814 — 22%** |

**Why it collapses**: 77.5% of DM token mass sits in the `80–150` and `150–250` bands, and delivery rate there is **30%** and **10%**. The bands that carry the tokens are the bands the model fails on.

**Latency cost**: ~628 model calls/day at p50 **3.7s** (p90 6.9s, max 14.0s) — roughly **38.6 minutes/day** added to the DM delivery path.

## 3. Safety held on every path

**No corruption reached delivery in any of the 40.**

Fail-closed fired on 40% (16/40), split about evenly:

| Reason | count |
|---|---|
| placeholder omitted / multiplicity | 4 |
| verify-tier literal dropped — `🫡`, `:7999`, `21,583`, `gate (a)` | 4 |

⇒ **Phi-4 drops ordinary literals, not just placeholders.** The two-tier design from Phase 1 is what catches the second group; a single-tier freeze protocol would have delivered those corrupted.

Bypass fired on 4 (10%), all the compressible-remainder floor (22–38 words against a 40-word minimum). Working as designed.

## 4. Independent confirmation of the longer-than-original defect

**5 of 20 delivered rewrites came back *longer* than the input** — −0.1% to −0.9% — passing every structural check, because nothing in the pipeline asked *"is it shorter?"*, only *"is it valid?"*

| band | gain | chars |
|---|---|---|
| 80–150 | −0.5% | 887 → 891 |
| 80–150 | −0.9% | 917 → 925 |
| 150–250 | −0.3% | 1,237 → 1,241 |
| 150–250 | −0.1% | 1,069 → 1,070 |
| 250+ | −0.5% | 1,718 → 1,727 |

Mr Radio found this independently in his own re-run and shipped the 5% minimum-gain gate (`4b8941cd`). **This run predates that gate**, so the two observations are independent rather than one echoing the other. Under his gate, 9 of 20 delivered rewrites would be rejected.

## 5. ⚠️ Caveats — this is a direction, not a verdict

1. **Per-band n is 10.** Too thin to carry a shipping decision.
2. **Ratios are character-based**, applied against token mass. Close, not identical — worth redoing in tokens.
3. **The 5% gate was applied in analysis**, not live in the run.
4. **One model, one prompt, one temperature.** Nothing here separates "Phi-4 can't do this" from "this prompt can't get Phi-4 to do it." A prompt that names the target ratio for the band, or few-shot examples at each length, is untested.

⇒ **Requested: a rerun at n=200 (50/band) through the in-repo harness.** It costs nothing on a local model, and it is the difference between a direction and a number anyone can act on.

## 6. What this does and does not say

**It does not say stop.** The safety kernel did exactly its job on every path, including two failure classes a single-tier design would have missed.

**It does say the shipping question changed.** The original question was *"can we find an endpoint cheap enough?"* — answered by going local. The question now is whether **22% of the projected saving justifies ~38 minutes/day of added delivery latency**, and whether a better prompt moves the long bands before that gets decided.

That is Rick's call, not ours.
