## The one-pixel-row finding (answer to Cheech's question A)

Cheech read the byte deltas as `3,840 = 1280 × 3` = three pixel rows at 1280 wide, and called
it environmental. **Conclusion right, arithmetic wrong** — and the correction strengthens it.

Verified by reading PNG IHDR headers of the actual baselines:

| baseline | dims | `w × h × 4` | junit pair |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fleet-status-populated.png` | 916 × 232 | 850,048 | 853,712 vs 850,048 |
| `multiplexer_phase5_notifications_pane.png` | 960 × 298 | 1,144,320 | 1,148,160 vs 1,144,320 |
| `multiplexer_phase6c_section_c_idle.png` | 960 × 201 | 771,840 | 775,680 vs 771,840 |
| `multiplexer_phase6c_section_c_filled.png` | 960 × 201 | 771,840 | 775,680 vs 771,840 |

- **No failing baseline is 1280 wide.** The one 1280-wide sibling in that family,
  `multiplexer_phase6c_section_c_ready_to_send.png`, **PASSED**.
- The comparator works in **RGBA (4 bytes/px)**: `3,840 = 960 × 4`, `3,664 = 916 × 4`.
- ⇒ every one of the four is **exactly ONE pixel row taller** than its baseline.

**Why this matters**: one row at *two different widths* (916 and 960) is much harder for a
content change to produce than a constant byte delta. Environmental, with more confidence
than the original reading, not less.

**Open hypothesis worth testing first thing**: the 3 height-tolerant tests survive the +1 row
by cropping to a common region; the other 9 may be **the same one-row shift** hitting
comparators that cannot absorb it. If so, 13 visual failures have **one** root cause.

## Second finding — the cancel endpoint gap (row 4b87fe61)

Holding `ts-3fc47888` exposed this. Verified from source, `src/cosa/rest/routers/queues.py`:

- **:1087-1090** — the handler resolves the job via `running_queue.get_by_id_hash()` **only**.
  So queued, scheduled-not-yet-queued, and deferred **all** return the same
  `404 "Job not found or not running: …"`. (Cheech had proven only the queued case.)
- **:1097-1098** — even for a RUNNING job it requires `isinstance( job, AgenticJobBase )`,
  else `400 "Only agentic jobs support cancellation"`. **A `test_suite` job is not one.**

⇒ For test-suite jobs `POST /api/jobs/{id}/cancel` has **no reachable success path at all** —
which is why the in-container process kill was the only lever anyone had found.

**The working lever** for a not-yet-started job:
