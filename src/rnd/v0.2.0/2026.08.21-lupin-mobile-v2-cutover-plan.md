## Verdict — it is smaller than the row said, and it splits into two waves

**13 production call sites, in exactly TWO files.** Not fourteen scattered across the app. Every
server call to a dying door funnels through two repository classes, which is the whole reason
this is a short job.

**And only TWO of the thirteen break when the 410s first go live.** `_retired_doors.py` retires
exactly two doors at this commit — `/api/push` and `/api/job-history/{job_id}/retry` — because
`/api/v2/submit` **has not been built yet** and *"a 410 that names a door that does not exist
yet is a refusal pointing at nothing"* (Cheech, 2026-08-21). The other eleven doors die later,
with step 11b.

⇒ **Wave 1 is two lines.** That is what has to land before the `:7999` bounce puts the
tombstones live on your box.

## Wave 1 — the two lines that break at step 11a

Both in `lib/features/queue/data/queue_repository.dart`:

| line | current | replacement |
|---|---|---|
| **17** | `_dio.post( '/api/push', data: req.toJson() )` | `/api/v2/ask` |
| **89** | `_dio.post( '/api/job-history/$jobId/retry', … )` | `/api/v2/ask` |

Both are **question-shaped**, which is exactly why they are the two that can retire today:
`/api/push` takes a bare question, and the retry handler pulls `question_text` off a stored row
and calls `push_job` with it. A bare question is what `/api/v2/ask` takes, and `ask` is live.

Comments and doc-strings naming the old paths sit at `queue_repository.dart:12`, `:83`, and
`queue_models.dart:16`, `:61` — cosmetic, but they are what the next reader believes.

**Request/response shape is the real work, not the URL string.** `/api/v2/ask` is synchronous
where `/api/push` was queue-and-poll. Whoever does this must read `v2_ask.py` and decide what
the repository returns to the BLoC — the URL swap alone will compile and then behave differently.

## Tests — 21 references across 4 files

`test/unit/queue/queue_repository_test.dart` · `test/unit/queue/queue_bloc_test.dart` ·
`test/unit/agentic/agentic_repository_test.dart` · `test/unit/agentic/agentic_submission_bloc_test.dart`

These are **mock handler keys** (`adapter.handlers[ 'POST /api/push' ]`), not live calls — they
hit no server and will not 410. They go red only when the production path string changes, so
they move **with** their wave, not before it.

🔴 **A test that only renames its mock key proves nothing.** If wave 1 changes the response shape
as well as the URL — and it does, `ask` is synchronous — then a mock updated to return the *old*
body shape under the *new* path will stay green while the app breaks. Update the fixture body,
not just the key.

## Sequencing — what actually has to happen before your bounce

Cheech ruled at 15:08 that merge-to-wip proceeds on green gates, but the **`:7999` bounce that
puts the 410s live waits for your word**. So the order is yours to set, and only wave 1 gates it:

1. Land wave 1 (two lines + their mocks) in this repo.
2. Then the bounce — mobile now speaks `ask` for both retired doors.
3. Wave 2 lands after step 11b builds `/api/v2/submit`; the eleven doors are alive until then.

**Nothing forces waves 1 and 2 to be one job**, and treating them as one is what made this look
like thirty-four edits before a bounce.

## How I measured this, so you can refute it

```bash
cd src/lupin-mobile
# production call sites
grep -rn "_dio\.\(post\|get\|put\|delete\)" lib --include="*.dart" -A2
# per-door, prod vs test
grep -rn "/api/push" lib test integration_test --include="*.dart" | grep -v push-agentic
```

**Scope of the sweep**: `lib/`, `test/`, `integration_test/` only — 279 Dart files. The repo also
vendors the Flutter SDK (7,216 `.dart` files in total); including it inflates every count and was
where the earlier broad numbers came from.

**Correction to row `44d91bf5`, which I wrote**: it says *"~14 call sites, ~20 tests"* and pairs
mobile with the Firefox plugin. The mobile figure is close (13 production, 21 test refs) but the
row implies a scattered surface; it is two files. **The Firefox half of that row is simply
wrong** — that repo calls no dying door at all. See the companion plan.
