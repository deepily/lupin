## The line, and the rule behind it

> ## "make me a podcast on KISS"

**Say "about" or "on" — and NAME KISS right after it.** Both halves, or it falls to the agent that never
opens your file. Don't say *from*, don't say *based on*, don't say *my document* without naming KISS.
