## The two numbers Rick ruled

| Parameter | Ruling | Was |
|---|---|---|
| **Fabrication denominator** | **narrow** — `fabrication_blocked / (fabrication_blocked + rewritten)` | `DENOMINATOR-TBD`; `FABRICATION_DENOMINATOR = None`, raising |
| **Operational discordant floor** | **10** | unset; `assert_floor_pre_stated` had no default |

Both are now stated. Neither is a code default: they are passed in at the call site and
recorded with the result, so a later run cannot quietly use different ones.

**The narrow denominator, spelled out**: the divisor is the rows where the tutor produced
a rewrite the guard then judged — the ones it delivered, plus the ones it refused for
fabricating. Rows refused for re-scoping or mislabelling, rows the gate rejected, and
`model_failed` rows are NOT under the line. Same choice for both arms.

**The floor of 10 is above the arithmetic minimum of 6.** Six is the point below which no
split can reach p < 0.05 (`b+c=5` → best case p = 0.0625). Rick's 10 is the
effect-size-worthy number, which is a stricter and separate judgement — the code enforces
both and refuses a floor below 6.

## The test

- **McNemar's EXACT test** — `scipy.stats.binomtest( min(b, c), b + c, 0.5 )`. Paired data;
  concordant rows carry no information about which model is more honest and are excluded
  by construction.
- **Wilson score interval**, closed-form from `scipy.stats.norm`, over `b / (b + c)`.
- **scipy version recorded at run time** from `scipy.__version__`, never copied from the
  pin. (`src/cosa/requirements.txt:210` pins 1.15.2; this box measures 1.17.1.)
- **statsmodels is not used and not installed.** Its `proportion_confint` defaults to the
  forbidden Wald interval.

## Latency is a TIEBREAKER, and only that

Mr. Radio, 15:03 EDT: the faster arm wins **on a statistical tie**. It is reported on every
run and decides nothing until McNemar comes back tied. Measured over rows where the model
actually answered — `model_failed` timings are a different kind of number.

**Rick ruled YES on the tiebreaker at 15:06 EDT, with two conditions** (both landed at
`500e2a1b`, in the payload rather than only here):

1. **It is a DEPLOYMENT comparison, not a claim about model speed.** Tiffany's point
   carried it: in production phi_4 is the LAN hop and flash_lite is the internet round
   trip, so both were measured exactly as they would be deployed. Move either arm to
   different infrastructure and the number no longer applies.
2. **p90 AND p99 per arm.** The internet leg is the variable one, and p99 is where it
   shows. The mechanism stands; the direction does not — see below.

What SURVIVES the withdrawal, because it is about method and not direction:

  - The one-definition percentile ruling (nearest-rank) and the divergence test.
  - The deployment-not-model-speed framing.
  - ⚠️ A p99 over 8 rows is near the maximum, not a tail estimate.

## What happens when the 400-row file lands

1. Pair on `frozen_index`, refusing a snapshot mismatch.
2. `discordant_counts` over `fabrication_blocked`.
3. `compare_arms( b, c, operational_floor=10 )` — which RAISES rather than reporting if
   `b + c < 10`.
4. Per-arm summaries under the **narrow** denominator, both arms the same.
5. Latency ratio reported beside them, as a tiebreaker only.

**Nothing above may be revised after seeing the data.** If the floor is not met, the
honest output is "the arms did not disagree often enough to say anything" — not a
loosened floor.
