### 3. `src/scripts/presentation-gate-silence-proof/prove_gate_silence.py` — correct check, overstated line

`require_lock_clear()` (`:396-411`) refuses to submit while `monopolize_inflight` or `monopolize_id`
is set. **This is a correct use.** It is not asking "is the venue free" — it asks "will Gate B defer
my foreign `pr-` job", and a held monopolize slot is exactly what does that
(`queue_consumer.py:106-124`). Its docstring says so.

The defect is one line of output, `:522`:

```python
print( f"[proof] :8000 lock clear (monopolize_id={ps.get( 'monopolize_id' )}) — safe to submit" )
```

**"safe to submit" is wider than what was checked.** The check licenses one narrow thing — this job
will not be Gate-B deferred — and the line reports general safety. A reader taking that sentence at
face value has been told the venue is free when only the monopolize slot was examined.

**Wording, not logic**, and recorded at its true weight: I expected a second broken caller here and
did not find one.

### 4. `src/scripts/observe_gate_b_admission.py` — correct, and looks wrong at a grep

It greps loudly for both fields, so it reads as a fourth misuse. It is not. It checks a **three-term
conjunction** — `monopolize_inflight` AND `inflight_agentic_jobs >= 1` AND `monopolize_id` names
**this run's own job** (`:252-262`) — to confirm its own job was admitted. That is an **identity**
question, which is precisely what the field answers.

### 5. `CLAUDE.md:341` + `CLAUDE.local.md:28-31` — already correct

The row lists the self-authorization rule as exposed. Read directly, it is not:

> `list-pending` **FIRST** to decide placement: empty queue → bounce + schedule + run now; something
> already **SCHEDULED** (queued, not yet running) → still self-authorized, but set `scheduled_at`
> AFTER the queued job; something **RUNNING** → queue behind it, no bounce.

It prescribes reading **the queue**, and already distinguishes the three states —
empty / queued / running — that the monopolize field cannot see. A seat following the written rule
was never reading `monopolize_id` at all.

**So the row's strongest sentence is too strong.** *"Every seat that has ever verified :8000 idle
verified nothing about queued work"* holds for anyone who used the **endpoint field**; it does not
hold for anyone who followed the **documented procedure**.
