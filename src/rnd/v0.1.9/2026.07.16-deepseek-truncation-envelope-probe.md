## 5. 🔑 REGION FINDING — deepseek serves ONLY at `global`

Discovered while hunting for a region that answers. **The honest oracle is a real prediction call** — the publisher-model metadata GET is the known liar (`metadata 200 ≠ servable`).

| Region | Result |
|---|---|
| `us-central1` | **400 FAILED_PRECONDITION** — *"Publisher model … is not available in region 'us-central1'."* |

| **`global`** | ✅ **200 — ANSWERED** |

⇒ **`deepseek-v3.2-maas` is `global`-only — the same pattern as `claude-opus-4-8`.**

⚠️ **Host gotcha (cost me a 404 of my own making)**: there is **no** `global-aiplatform.googleapis.com`. The global endpoint is served from **`aiplatform.googleapis.com`** with `locations/global`. Every other region uses `{region}-aiplatform.googleapis.com`.

**Note the error bodies are READINGS, not just failures** — `us-central1`'s `FAILED_PRECONDITION` names the exact reason, and `us-west1`'s 404 names the model. *A refused write can be a READ.*
