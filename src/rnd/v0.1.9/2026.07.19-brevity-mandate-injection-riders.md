## Design decisions (task said implementer's call)

| Q | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) inbound only, or echo on send? | **Inbound only** | Leverage is at reply-composition time. The sender has already composed; a rider there is pure cost. |
| (b) suppress after first DM in thread? | **No suppression** | Hooks are stateless per-message; thread-state tracking costs more than the 103 bytes it saves. |
| (c) `dm_send` body assembly or render layer? | **`build_peer_dm_reminder`** | Already the single source for both delivery paths — no drift between them. |
| (d) *not asked* — one-way advisories? | **Suppressed** | An arbiter advisory takes no reply (bug `8894e597`); a reply-shaping rider there is pure byte cost. |

## The two load-bearing constraints

**1. Net-smaller payload.** Maria flagged the collision with the pre-existing micro-prompt-reduction
ask: that item SHRINKS the STT preamble, this one ADDS to it. Landing this as a straight addition
would have grown the exact thing Rick asked to shrink. Resolved by re-cutting the rider in the same
edit — measured, not asserted, and pinned by `test_voice_ack_rider_stays_under_byte_ceiling` (<260)
so it cannot quietly regrow.

**2. "ONLY WHEN ASKED".** Never "when the content requires it" — a self-assessed exception hands
length-discretion back to the verbose author. Pinned by
`test_escape_clause_wording_is_asked_not_content_requires`, which asserts `"content requires"` is
absent from the tag. A code comment on `BREVITY_TAG` says do not soften it.
