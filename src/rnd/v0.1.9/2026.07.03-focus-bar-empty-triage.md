## Suspect (b) — ELIMINATED firsthand

**Hypothesis**: the strip filter `cc_hide_inactive_strip` (`notifications_cc_hide_inactive_strip`) hides icons for sessions with NO allocated voice persona; if the 3h outage tripped the host-side voice-persona stale-prune, the strip would hide the whole fleet.

**Check** (2026-07-03 ~05:57Z, firsthand, `~/.claude/sessions/cc-*.json`): all **11 active fleet personas carry an intact `voice_persona` dict** with fresh mtimes — Cheech, Clayton, Krishna, Rachel, Rio, Tiberius, Tiffany, arnold, maria, mr radio, sam. (9 NULL-persona bridges also present, but those are old dead-PID sessions with mtime ages 85–954 min — correctly pruned days/hours ago, not the active fleet.)

**Why the outage didn't strip them**: `prune_dead_persona_bridges()` (`session_bridge.py:1624`) nulls `voice_persona` **only when the host PID is DEAD** (`_is_pid_alive()` False) — it is **PID-liveness-based, not time-based**. The independent mtime-TTL guard (`find_active_sessions`, `stale_threshold_seconds` default **43200s = 12h**) also would not fire on a 3h window. The active fleet's processes stayed alive through the idle pause → personas untouched.

**Conclusion**: personas intact ⇒ `cc_hide_inactive_strip` is NOT the cause. (b) does not contribute.
