## 3. Root causes → one fix family (all one arbiter-window work package)

The afternoon exposed a **routing asymmetry**: liveness infra erred **LOUD on parked managers** and **QUIET on a held worker** simultaneously.

1. **Staleness clock ignores honored holds + declared future chase.** Mr. Radio's hourly chase cadence (matched to Rick's gates) sits just past the 2700s threshold → it pokes at :45 every hour *by construction*. **Fix**: extend the staleness clock to the declared `next_chase_ts` when the hold is honored + `awaiting user:*`. (Bug `285c0343`, direction (a).)
2. **Last-signal velocity ignored.** Advancing last-signal ⇒ alive ⇒ suppress/stretch. (Bug `285c0343`, direction (b).)
3. **`-r2` duplicate re-delivery.** Advisory dedupe + a resolvable reply address (dups arrived with `recipient_unresolved` "a peer session"). (`285c0343` companion.)
4. **Under-wake twin — `d1bb1456`** (DM doesn't wake a held worker). Fixing it removes the *only* reason the one "helpful" stale poke was needed.
5. **Oracle count fidelity + mtime-anchored hold-freshness.** The oracle must read the store truthfully (kills Mr. Radio's fabricated counts) AND anchor hold-freshness to file mtime (catches Clayton's *expired* hold correctly while sparing Mr. Radio's *fresh* one). The differentiator that separates a true positive from a false one is **hold-freshness + store-count truth, not the poke class**.
6. **Log-rotation `a2ca6f9d`** + the pending `:8001` restart batch.
7. **Empty `notifications.db`** — advisories aren't durably queryable; they survive only as hook delivery-receipts. Worth wiring or pruning.
