## 3. WHERE THE STATE LIVES — the design's real trap, and it already has a precedent

… A client-only toggle is THEATRE."* Correct, and the receipt is §1: **every one of the nineteen

button in one tab would have changed nothing about that day.

**Copy `flow_ratio_settings.py` exactly.** It is the same problem already solved, for the slider the
toggle sits next to:

| | |
|---|---|
| **file** | `<LUPIN_FLOW_RATIO_DIR or fleet_data_root()>/flow-ratio/…` — a JSON override **outside the repo** |
| **why outside** | `git clean -xdf` would take it, and `git status` would keep offering it |

## 5. WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE — the sketch proper

```
┌─ Flow gate ────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Window   [====|--------]  24h                             │
│  Threshold[======|------]  1.20                            │
│                                                             │
│  ⏸  Manager promotions   ( ● OFF )   for [ 30 min ▾ ]       │
│      Paused by Rick · 22 min left · 3 promotions refused    │
│                                              [ Resume now ] │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Four properties, each earning its place from §1:**

1. **It is a PAUSE, not a permission.** Label it in time, not in authority — *"Manager promotions:
   paused 22 min"*, never *"managers may not promote."* Nothing forbidden happened on 09-05; a
   manager did exactly what he was told, at a speed nobody had thought about. **An attention
   instrument names the clock. A permission system names the person.**
2. **It reports what it cost.** *"3 promotions refused"* is the whole difference between an
   instrument and a switch. A pause with no counter is indistinguishable from a pause nobody hit —
   the same defect this repo files under *an empty result is two different failures wearing one
   face*. **The counter is the evidence the switch did anything at all.**
3. **It shows its own expiry, and resuming is one click.** A timer the operator can see is a timer
   he will not be surprised by.
4. **It says who.** One line, one name. When a manager is refused, the refusal quotes the same name
   back — so a worker never has to guess whether the fleet is broken or deliberately quiet.

## 5a. THE OPEN CHOICE — hard OFF versus rate limit

Both would have gated the 09-05 burst. **They are different products and the difference is who
decides.**

| | **hard OFF** (this sketch's shape) | **rate limit** |
|---|---|---|
| who acts | **Rick**, deliberately, when he wants focus | **nobody** — it fires on its own |
| when it fires | only while he has it on | every burst, forever, including ones he wants |
| the 09-05 burst | blocked, **because he chose to block it** | blocked, **including his own "clear them all"** |
| failure mode | ⚠️ **he leaves it on** — hence the TTL in §4.5 | ⚠️ **it throttles work he ordered**, silently, and there is no operator to appeal to |
| matches his words? | ✅ *"turn it off **momentarily**"*, *"tasks **I** deem most important"* | ❌ nothing he said asks for an automatic governor |
| cost | a flag, a TTL, a consult inside `approval_for_promotion` | the same, **plus** a window, a counter per actor, and a policy for what a refused-then-retried promotion does |

**Recommendation: hard OFF.** Two reasons, and only the second is a measurement. (1) It is what he
described — *momentarily*, and *what I deem most important*, are both operator verbs. (2) §1d shows
the one burst in the record was **Rick's own instruction being executed**; an automatic governor
would have fought him, and he would have had no way to turn it off.

⚠️ **The strongest case for the rate limit, stated fairly rather than strawmanned**: a hard OFF only
helps when Rick is *watching* and thinks to flip it. He was not watching on 09-05 — the burst is why
he asked for this at all. **A switch nobody flips prevents nothing.** If the real requirement is
*"never let a manager fire ten asks at me in nine minutes, whether or not I am paying attention"*,
then the rate limit is the correct product and the hard OFF is the wrong one.

⇒ 🔴 **THIS IS RICK'S CALL, NOT MINE AND NOT THE FLEET'S**, because the two answer different
questions: *"let me impose focus when I choose"* versus *"protect my attention when I am not
looking."* **The data does not choose between them.** Both are buildable on the same server-side
seam, so the choice is reversible and neither forecloses the other — a hard OFF shipped first can
gain a rate limit later without moving where the state lives.
