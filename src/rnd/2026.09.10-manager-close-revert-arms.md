## R8 and R9 — what changed after the review

- **R8** moves a widened carve-out above the won't-fix clause. At `64fbea55` it reddened the door test `test_a_manager_seat_still_cannot_WONT_FIX_a_HELD_row`.
- **R9** keys the carve-out on manager-hood alone. At `64fbea55` it reddened the promote, demote, un-park and parked-close door tests.
- **Why the door tests went blind**: at `6e9d49be` the router runs the manager check only for a close or a manager-key claim. A promote, demote, un-park or won't-fix therefore reaches `refusal_for_admission` with `closer_is_manager=False`, and a wrong carve-out cannot open them at the door. In the run at `6e9d49be`, both were still caught, but not by the tests named for them: R8 only by a single combined pure test, through its promote assertion; R9 by that test and by the parked-close door test.
- **The remedy chosen**: a direct test of the gate function, one per clause, with `closer_is_manager=True` (`90c08641`). A door test cannot reach these clauses any more, because the door never hands the gate a manager on those moves. Of the "still cannot" door tests, `test_a_manager_seat_still_cannot_close_a_PARKED_row` is the only one that still reaches the carve-out with a manager, because a parked close does run the check; R9 reddens it.
- R1–R10 were first run at `64fbea55`, off a 448-passed baseline, and all ten were killed there. R11 and R12 were added for the review change.
- **R13 is Mr. Radio's arm X**: `manager_close = closer_is_manager`, dropping its `to_status == done` half. It survived every test at `90c08641` and is not equivalent. The manager check also runs when a request claims the manager key, so a logged-in approver promoting a held row with `manager_attestation` in its receipts arrives with `closer_is_manager=True`, and under the mutant that promote skips the promotion gate and the throttle. Two door tests now drive that caller and move; R13 reddens exactly those two.
