## 2. What went wrong, and what to do next time

1. **I merged `d510c2e11` without running the reuse gate, and it carried a new definition with no outcome line** (draw_sample). The working-branch gate does not include the reuse gate, so nothing stopped it; Rachel found it on her next gate run. Next time: run `src/tests/run-reuse-gate.sh` beside the census before every merge that adds a definition. It takes seconds.
2. **A decision row left in progress after its decision was taken blocked a peer for hours**. The parent decision row stayed open after Rick lifted his first ruling on 23 Nov. Cheech's reuse-review row was blocked on it. The arbiter sent about a dozen "blocking Cheech" nudges before I looked at what it was keyed on. Next time: close a decision row when the decision is taken, and move the build onto its own rows. When the arbiter says "blocking X", run `task_query( blocked_by_persona=me )` and the owner's blocked rows first.
3. **Rick could not act on cards he did not understand**. He declined the mobile un-park card and asked what "folding the forecast" means. Next time: plain words on every card (memory `feedback_no_word_fold_for_forecast.md`). Once the row was retitled in plain words, he moved it in one click.
4. **A handoff raced the worker**. I moved the duplicate fixes from Tiffany to Krishna after she had already committed them (`af9a3e37f`), and her version also edited six old tests. Krishna built on her commit instead of starting over. Next time: ask a seat what it has committed before reassigning its row.
