## 3. What went wrong (fix)

1. **A recorded value was taken for the value used.** The ledger named binary 2.1.316, but the SDK ran its bundled 2.1.92. Six sibling runners set the transport from `--cli-path`. Cost: one failed launch, zero judge spend (`4fec38d69`).
2. **The 25 Oct labelling run saved each pair's code text but not its commit.** Matching lost 71 of 157 pairs. Recovery needed an index rebuilt at an inferred commit (`1e606a726`) and a card to Rick. 52 pairs stay out anyway. The writers now refuse without a commit (`60293bceb`).
3. **The plan-only form passed what the live step refused.** Attempt b's plan gave 315 posts. Twin pairs with identical text collapsed onto one record. The live step found 6 repeated bodies after 131 draft calls: the guard worked, after the spend.
4. **The count of judged pairs changed three times in a day.** It read 86, then 105, then 97, each for a different population. The headline once called the population "judged".
5. **A decision card used terms Rick did not have.** Rick said he had no idea what "the fixed rule" was. The card carried the manager's reading, which the mark owner had overruled. The explainer came after.
6. **The census was skipped twice more.** A unit-only review passed a mutable default at 11:46. My own first commit left three tests broken (`578f08b74`). Part 2's fourth ruling did not stop it.
7. **A claim ran ahead of its receipt.** A defect asserted at 16:13 was withdrawn at 16:26. A condensed DM also lost figures, and the file-path fix lives in one memento.
