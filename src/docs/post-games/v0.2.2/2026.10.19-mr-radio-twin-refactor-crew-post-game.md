## 6. Lessons for the failure-mode catalog

- **Search for a landed helper before a slice builds one.** Peers landed helpers after the census sha the brief was cut from.
- **Test a widened guard with a hollow delegate.** A guard widened to allow delegation also passes when the delegate does nothing.
- **Check every ledger sha for a verdict before building the landing tree.** During the build is too late to staff the review calmly.
- **Ask for a memento before a seat goes idle overnight.** A seat idle for hours becomes unreachable, and its reap is then done blind.
- **A held row listed for the operator is a gate.** Fire the targeted ask, one per decision, rather than listing it in a status.
